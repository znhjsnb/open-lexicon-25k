🏛️ 来源历史:
该词在美国英语中由 jackrabbit“北美野兔”和 antelope“羚羊”混成。给兔子标本装上鹿角的恶作剧和旅游纪念品，使这种虚构动物的形象广为流传。
The word was coined in American English by blending jackrabbit and antelope. Hoax taxidermy mounts and tourist souvenirs helped popularize the imaginary antlered rabbit.
🧩 构词衍生:
混成构词：jack(rabbit) + (ante)lope → jackalope；这些截取部分不是独立的英语词根或词缀。复数为 jackalopes，没有常用的派生词族。

⚖️ 近义微雕:

mucormycosis 1 词性 · 双语对齐

n. 名词 //ˌmjuːkɔːrmaɪˈkoʊsɪs// 毛霉病，由毛霉目真菌引起的感染 (an infection caused by fungi belonging to the order Mucorales)
COBUILD: Mucormycosis is a serious infection caused by certain molds, which can affect areas such as the sinuses, lungs, or skin.
搭配: invasive mucormycosis; pulmonary mucormycosis 侵袭性毛霉病；肺毛霉病
例句: Laboratory tests confirmed a diagnosis of pulmonary mucormycosis.
实验室检查确诊了肺毛霉病。
🏛️ 来源历史:
mucormycosis 结合了与拉丁语 mucor“霉”有关的成分和 mycosis“真菌病”；后者包含源自希腊语的“真菌”词根。名称指毛霉目真菌引起的感染，并不限于毛霉属这一属的真菌。
Mucormycosis combines an element associated with Latin mucor, “mold,” and mycosis, “fungal disease,” whose fungal root comes from Greek. The name denotes infection by fungi of the order Mucorales, rather than exclusively by the genus Mucor.
🧩 构词衍生:
mucor（毛霉；霉）+ myc-（真菌）+ -osis（病理状态）→ mucormycosis。复数：mucormycoses；相关术语：Mucor（毛霉属）、Mucorales（毛霉目）、mycosis（真菌病）、mycotic（真菌感染的）；没有常用派生动词。分级说明：下列 CEFR-C2 是编辑性难度估计，并非经过核实的官方词项等级。
Mucor (“mold”) + myc- (“fungus”) + -osis (“a pathological condition”) → mucormycosis. The plural is mucormycoses; related terms include Mucor, Mucorales, mycosis, and mycotic. There is no common derived verb. Classification note: CEFR-C2 below is an editorial difficulty estimate, not a verified official level for this word.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
Mucorales infection“毛霉目真菌感染”是接近同义的描述性表达；zygomycosis“接合菌病”是较旧且范围更广的名称，不宜严格等同；black fungus“黑真菌病”是部分媒体使用的非正式称呼，容易误导，并非严谨同义词。
Mucorales infection is a close descriptive equivalent; zygomycosis is an older, broader term and is not strictly interchangeable; black fungus is an informal media label that can mislead and is not a precise medical synonym.
