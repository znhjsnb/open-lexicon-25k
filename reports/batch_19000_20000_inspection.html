mycelium 1 词性 · 双语对齐
n. 名词 //maɪˈsiːliəm// 菌丝体；由菌丝构成的真菌营养体 (The network of hyphae forming the vegetative body of a fungus.)
COBUILD: A mycelium is a mass of fine, branching threads through which a fungus grows and absorbs nutrients.
搭配: fungal mycelium; mycelium growth 真菌菌丝体；菌丝体生长
例句: The mycelium spread through the damp wood and absorbed nutrients as the wood decomposed.
菌丝体在潮湿的木材中蔓延，并在木材分解过程中吸收养分。
🏛️ 来源历史:
该词借自新拉丁语，词源核心是希腊语 mykēs，意为“蘑菇、真菌”。它成为真菌学术语，用来指构成真菌营养体的菌丝集合。
The word comes from New Latin and ultimately contains Greek mykēs, meaning “mushroom” or “fungus.” It became a mycological term for the collective threads forming a fungus's vegetative body.
🧩 构词衍生:
现代英语中宜将 mycelium 作为完整的专业词基记忆，其内部不宜机械拆成常用英语词缀。复数为 mycelia；形容词 mycelial（菌丝体的）可记为 myceli- + -al；相关构词成分 myco- 表示“真菌”，见 mycology（真菌学），但 mycology 不是 mycelium 的直接派生词。
In modern English, mycelium is best learned as a complete technical base rather than mechanically divided into productive English affixes. Its plural is mycelia; the adjective mycelial can be analyzed as myceli- + -al. The related combining form myco- means “fungus,” as in mycology, which is a related word rather than a direct derivative of mycelium.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
hyphal network（菌丝网络）强调网状结构，常可用来解释 mycelium；fungal network（真菌网络）表达较宽泛，未必严格指菌丝体；spawn（菌种）指用于接种的繁殖材料，常含菌丝体及其培养基，不能与 mycelium 完全互换。
Hyphal network emphasizes the network structure and often paraphrases mycelium; fungal network is broader and less technically precise; spawn means material used to inoculate a substrate and often includes both mycelium and its growing medium.
💡 秒记锚点:
myc- 联想到真菌：蘑菇是显眼的部分，菌丝体是延伸取食的细丝网络。
Connect myc- with fungus: the mushroom is the visible structure, while the mycelium is the spreading network that feeds it.

n. 名词 //ˌmaɪəʊˈɡləʊbɪn// 肌红蛋白；肌肉中结合并储存氧的含血红素蛋白质 (An oxygen-binding, heme-containing protein in muscle.)
COBUILD: Myoglobin is a protein in muscle cells that binds oxygen and helps keep it available for the muscles to use.
搭配: myoglobin concentration; myoglobin release 肌红蛋白浓度；肌红蛋白释放
例句: Myoglobin helps muscle cells maintain a supply of oxygen during activity.
肌红蛋白帮助肌细胞在活动时维持氧的供应。
🏛️ 来源历史:
该词由 myo-（肌肉）与 globin（珠蛋白）组合而成，其中 myo- 源于希腊语 mys，意为“肌肉”。名称体现了它与血红蛋白的蛋白质组分之间的联系，并指出其主要存在于肌肉中。
The word combines myo-, meaning “muscle,” from Greek mys, with globin, the protein component associated with hemoglobin. The name identifies an analogous oxygen-binding protein found in muscle.
🧩 构词衍生:
myo-（肌肉）+ globin（珠蛋白）→ myoglobin。常用作不可数名词；myoglobins 可指不同种类的肌红蛋白。相关派生词和复合词包括 myoglobinemia / myoglobinaemia（肌红蛋白血症）、myoglobinuria（肌红蛋白尿）、myoglobinuric（肌红蛋白尿的）、oxymyoglobin（氧合肌红蛋白）、deoxymyoglobin（脱氧肌红蛋白）和 metmyoglobin（高铁肌红蛋白）。
Myo- (“muscle”) + globin → myoglobin. It is usually uncountable, although myoglobins can refer to different forms. Related derivatives and compounds include myoglobinemia / myoglobinaemia, myoglobinuria, myoglobinuric, oxymyoglobin, deoxymyoglobin, and metmyoglobin.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
该词没有常用的严格同义词。hemoglobin（血红蛋白）主要在红细胞中运输氧；globin（珠蛋白）指这类含血红素蛋白的蛋白质部分或相关蛋白家族；oxymyoglobin（氧合肌红蛋白）专指已结合氧的肌红蛋白。
There is no common exact synonym. Hemoglobin mainly transports oxygen in red blood cells; globin denotes the protein component of such heme proteins or the related protein family; oxymyoglobin specifically means myoglobin with oxygen bound to it.
💡 秒记锚点:
myo- 是“肌肉”：把 myoglobin 记成肌肉里的“氧气储备蛋白”。
Myo- means “muscle”: picture myoglobin as the muscle's oxygen reserve protein.

n. 名词 //ˌmɪθəˈpiːə// 神话创作；神话的形成或构建 (The creation or formation of myths.)
COBUILD: Mythopoeia is the activity or process of creating myths, especially a connected body of myths for an imagined world.
搭配: literary mythopoeia; Tolkien's mythopoeia 文学中的神话创作；托尔金的神话创作
例句: Through mythopoeia, the novelist gave her imagined world its own gods, legends, and creation stories.
这位小说家通过神话创作，为她虚构的世界赋予了独有的神祇、传说和创世故事。
🏛️ 来源历史:
该词源自希腊语 mythopoiia，意为“神话创作”，由 mythos（故事、神话）与表示“制作、创造”的成分组成，后者与 poiein（制作）同源。现代文学批评沿用这一核心含义，尤其用来描述作家构建完整神话体系的活动。
The word comes from Greek mythopoiia, meaning “myth-making,” combining mythos, “story” or “myth,” with a formation related to poiein, “to make.” Modern literary criticism retains this meaning, especially for the construction of an interconnected mythology.
🧩 构词衍生:
mytho-（神话）+ -poeia（创作、形成）→ mythopoeia，通常作不可数名词。主要相关词有 mythopoeic（创作神话的）、mythopoeically（以神话创作的方式）、mythopoetic（神话创作的；具有神话创造力的）、mythopoetically（以神话创作的方式）、mythopoet（神话创作者）以及近义名词 mythopoesis（神话创作或形成）。
Mytho- (“myth”) + -poeia (“making” or “formation”) → mythopoeia, usually an uncountable noun. Principal related forms include mythopoeic, mythopoeically, mythopoetic, mythopoetically, mythopoet, and the closely related noun mythopoesis.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
myth-making（神话创造）最直白，既可指文学创作，也可指现实中的神话化叙事；mythopoesis（神话形成或创作）与 mythopoeia 很接近，但更偏学术表达；world-building（世界构建）范围更广，还包括地理、制度和文化，不限于神话。
Myth-making is the plainest equivalent and can describe both literary invention and the creation of idealized public narratives; mythopoesis is a close, more academic equivalent; world-building is broader, covering geography, institutions, and cultures as well as myths.
💡 秒记锚点:
mytho- 是“神话”，-poeia 是“创造”：把零散故事创造为一套神话世界。
Mytho- means “myth” and -poeia means “making”: turn individual stories into a connected mythic world.
photomultiplier 1 词性 · 双语对齐
n. 名词 //ˌfəʊtəʊˈmʌltɪplaɪə// 光电倍增管；将微弱光转换为电信号并通过电子倍增加以放大的探测器 (A detector that converts weak light into an electrical signal and amplifies it through electron multiplication.)
COBUILD: A photomultiplier is a very sensitive device that detects light by converting it into electrons and multiplying those electrons to produce a measurable electrical signal.
搭配: photomultiplier tube; photomultiplier gain 光电倍增管；光电倍增管增益
例句: The photomultiplier detected the faint flashes of light produced inside the instrument.
光电倍增管探测到了仪器内部产生的微弱闪光。
🏛️ 来源历史:
该词是在光电探测技术发展过程中，由 photo-（光）与 multiplier（倍增器）组合而成的技术名称。photo- 源于希腊语 phōs（光）；名称中的“倍增”指光产生的电子经过逐级倍增，使微弱光信号变得可测量。
The term was formed during the development of photoelectric detection by combining photo-, “light,” with multiplier. Photo- comes from Greek phōs, “light”; multiplication refers to the successive amplification of electrons released by incoming light.
🧩 构词衍生:
photo-（光）+ multiplier（倍增器）→ photomultiplier；multiplier 又由 multiply（倍增）去掉词尾 y、改为 i 后加 -er 构成。复数为 photomultipliers；常见组合有 photomultiplier tube，缩写 PMT；相关过程名词为 photomultiplication（光电倍增），并可与 photoelectric（光电的）、photodetector（光探测器）联系记忆。
Photo- (“light”) + multiplier → photomultiplier; multiplier is formed from multiply + -er, with final y changing to i. The plural is photomultipliers. The common expression photomultiplier tube is abbreviated PMT; photomultiplication names the related process. Photoelectric and photodetector are useful related formations.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
photomultiplier tube（光电倍增管，PMT）是通常可互换的完整名称；electron multiplier（电子倍增器）强调电子倍增部分，本身不一定包含光电转换；photodetector（光探测器）是上位词，还包括光电二极管等其他器件。
Photomultiplier tube, or PMT, is the usual fuller equivalent; electron multiplier emphasizes electron amplification and need not include conversion from light; photodetector is the broader category, which also includes devices such as photodiodes.
💡 秒记锚点:
photo- 光 → 产生电子 → multiplier 倍增电子：微弱光变成可测电信号。
Photo-: light releases electrons; multiplier: those electrons multiply, turning faint light into a measurable signal.
picosecond 1 词性 · 双语对齐
n. 名词 //ˈpiːkəʊˌsekənd// 皮秒；万亿分之一秒，即 10⁻¹² 秒 (One trillionth of a second, or 10⁻¹² seconds.)
COBUILD: A picosecond is a unit of time equal to one millionth of a millionth of a second.
搭配: picosecond laser; picosecond pulse 皮秒激光器；皮秒脉冲
例句: Each laser pulse lasted only a few picoseconds.
每个激光脉冲仅持续几皮秒。
🏛️ 来源历史:
该词属于现代国际科学词汇，由 pico- 与 second（秒）组合而成；pico- 于 1960 年被正式采用为表示 10⁻¹² 的国际单位制词头。second 的时间单位义源于中世纪拉丁语，原指小时经过第二次六十分割后得到的单位。
The word belongs to modern international scientific vocabulary and combines pico- with second; pico- was formally adopted as the SI prefix for 10⁻¹² in 1960. The time-unit sense of second comes from medieval Latin terminology for the second successive division of an hour into sixtieths.
🧩 构词衍生:
pico-（10⁻¹²）+ second（秒）→ picosecond（皮秒）；复数为 picoseconds，单位符号为 ps。该名词可作定语，如 picosecond pulses（皮秒脉冲），这不构成独立的常用形容词词性；相关词包括 picosecond-scale（皮秒尺度的）、nanosecond（纳秒）和 femtosecond（飞秒）。
Pico- (10⁻¹²) + second → picosecond; the plural is picoseconds and the unit symbol is ps. The noun can modify another noun, as in picosecond pulses, without constituting a separate common adjective. Related formations include picosecond-scale, nanosecond, and femtosecond.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
one trillionth of a second（万亿分之一秒）是准确的释义性同义表达，ps 是单位符号；nanosecond（纳秒）等于 1,000 皮秒；femtosecond（飞秒）等于千分之一皮秒，后二者是相邻量级单位，不能作为同义词替换。
One trillionth of a second is an exact explanatory equivalent, and ps is the unit symbol. A nanosecond equals 1,000 picoseconds, whereas a femtosecond equals one thousandth of a picosecond; these neighboring units are related terms rather than synonyms.
💡 秒记锚点:
pico- 锁定 10⁻¹²：一秒分成一万亿份，一份就是一皮秒。
Fix pico- to 10⁻¹²: divide one second into a trillion equal parts, and one part is a picosecond.

🧩 构词衍生:

In modern English, scrofula is learned as a whole medical stem; related derivatives use scroful-: scroful- + -ous → scrofulous, meaning affected by or relating to scrofula, or figuratively morally corrupt. Further derivatives are scrofulously and scrofulousness, both very uncommon.

tuberculous lymphadenitis（结核性淋巴结炎）是更明确的医学名称；cervical tuberculous lymphadenitis（颈部结核性淋巴结炎）明确限定颈部；the king's evil（王病）是该病的历史称呼。scrofula 本身带有传统病名色彩。
Tuberculous lymphadenitis is the more explicit medical term; cervical tuberculous lymphadenitis specifies the neck; the king's evil is a historical name for the condition. Scrofula itself has the character of a traditional disease name.
💡 秒记锚点:
把这个旧病名锚定在“颈部肿大的淋巴结”，再联想 scrofulous 表示“与该病有关的”。
Anchor this old disease name to swollen neck lymph nodes, then connect scrofulous with being affected by the disease.
specular 1 词性 · 双语对齐
CEFR-C2
adj. 形容词 //ˈspekjələ// 镜面般的；镜面反射的 (Mirrorlike, or relating to reflection in a definite direction from a smooth surface.)
COBUILD: A specular surface reflects light like a mirror, and specular reflection sends light in a definite direction rather than scattering it widely.
搭配: specular reflection; specular highlights 镜面反射；镜面高光
例句: The polished metal produced bright specular highlights under the studio lights.
在摄影棚灯光的照射下，抛光金属表面出现了明亮的镜面高光。
🏛️ 来源历史:
Specular 借自拉丁语 specularis，源于 speculum（镜子），而 speculum 又与表示“看”的 specere 有关。它在英语中的含义从“与镜子有关的”发展为描述镜面般表面及其定向反射。
Specular comes from Latin specularis, formed from speculum, meaning mirror, which is related to specere, meaning to look. In English, its meaning developed from relating to mirrors to describing mirrorlike surfaces and their directional reflection.
🧩 构词衍生:
可分析为源自 speculum（镜子）的词干 specul- + -ar（……的）；specul- 是拉丁来源的黏着词干，并非现代独立单词。主要词族为 specular（镜面的）、specularly（以镜面反射方式）、specularity（镜面反射性质或程度）；speculum（窥器；反射镜）是相关名词。
It can be analyzed as specul-, the stem associated with speculum, meaning mirror, + the adjective-forming suffix -ar; specul- is a Latin-derived bound stem rather than an independent modern word. The main family includes specular, specularly, and specularity; speculum, meaning a medical viewing instrument or a reflecting mirror, is a related noun.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
mirrorlike（镜面般的）是最直接的日常近义表达；reflective（反光的）范围更广，不一定形成清晰的镜面反射；glossy（有光泽的）强调外观明亮光滑。specular 更精确地描述镜面式的定向反射，常见于光学与计算机图形学。
Mirrorlike is the closest everyday equivalent; reflective is broader and does not necessarily imply mirrorlike reflection; glossy emphasizes a shiny, smooth appearance. Specular more precisely describes directional, mirrorlike reflection and is common in optics and computer graphics.
💡 秒记锚点:
speculum＝镜子；specular＝光像照镜子一样被反射。
Speculum means mirror; specular means that light reflects as it does from a mirror.
