COBUILD: Deuterium is a stable form of hydrogen whose atoms have one proton and one neutron in the nucleus.

🏛️ 来源历史:
deuterium 是在20世纪30年代发现这种氢同位素后创造的名称，以希腊语 deuteros（第二）为基础，加上科学命名后缀 -ium。“第二”关联其质量数2，即原子核中共有两个核子。
Deuterium was coined after the discovery of this hydrogen isotope in the early 1930s, using Greek deuteros, meaning second, and the scientific naming suffix -ium. Second refers to its mass number of two, reflecting the two nucleons in its nucleus.
🧩 构词衍生:
构词：deuter-（第二）+ -ium（科学名称后缀）→ deuterium；它是氢的同位素名称，并非另一种化学元素。主要相关词族：deuteron（氘核）、deuterate（使氘化）、deuterated（氘化的）、deuteration（氘化）、deuteride（氘化物）；deuteriate、deuteriated、deuteriation 是相应的较少见变体。
Formation: deuter- meaning second + the scientific naming suffix -ium produces deuterium; it names an isotope of hydrogen, not a separate chemical element. Principal related forms include deuteron, deuterate, deuterated, deuteration, and deuteride; deuteriate, deuteriated, and deuteriation are less common variants.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
heavy hydrogen（重氢）是通俗同义名称，hydrogen-2（氢-2）是明确标示质量数的同位素名称，²H 则是核素符号；三者指向同一种氢同位素，区别主要在表达方式和使用场合。
Heavy hydrogen is a descriptive synonym, hydrogen-2 explicitly states the isotope's mass number, and ²H is its nuclide symbol. All refer to the same hydrogen isotope; the difference is chiefly in notation and context.
💡 秒记锚点:

Deuter- means second: one proton plus one neutron makes two nucleons, so remember hydrogen-2.

Impregnable entered English through French imprenable, meaning impossible to capture, combining a negative prefix with prenable, from prendre, to take. It described defenses that an enemy could not capture before extending to unassailable positions and arguments.
🧩 构词衍生:
现代分析：im-（不）+ pregnable（可攻取的，罕用）→ impregnable；不要把中间部分拆成与 pregnant（怀孕的）有关的词根。主要派生词：impregnability（不可攻克性；不可动摇性）、impregnably（以不可攻克或不可动摇的方式）；impregnableness 是较少见的名词形式。
Modern analysis: im- meaning not + pregnable, a rare adjective meaning capable of being captured, produces impregnable; its middle portion should not be analyzed as a root related to pregnant. Principal derivatives are impregnability and impregnably; impregnableness is a less common noun.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
invincible（不可战胜的）强调在战斗或竞争中不会被击败；unassailable（无懈可击的）尤其适合地位、证据或论点；impenetrable（无法穿透的）强调不能进入或穿过，而 impregnable 的核心是“拿不下、攻不破”。
Invincible emphasizes being unbeatable in combat or competition. Unassailable is especially suitable for positions, evidence, or arguments. Impenetrable emphasizes resistance to entry or passage, while impregnable centers on resistance to capture or defeat.

In modern English, rococo is an unanalyzed French borrowing used as both a noun and an adjective; it should not be forced into an English root-and-suffix analysis. Uremia or uraemia shares the historical root referring to urine but is not directly derived from urea.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
carbamide（尿素；脲）是同一化合物的技术名称；carbonyl diamide（碳酰二胺）突出其化学结构；urea 是生理学、农业和一般说明中最常用的名称。
Carbamide is a technical name for the same compound; carbonyl diamide emphasizes its chemical structure; urea is the usual name in physiology, agriculture, and general descriptions.
💡 秒记锚点:
名称连着“尿”，用途连着“肥”：尿液中的含氮物，也是含氮肥料。
Link the name to urine and the use to fertilizer: the same nitrogen-containing compound connects both.
artiste 1 词性 · 双语对齐
CEFR-C2
n. 名词 //ɑːˈtiːst// 专业表演艺人，尤指歌手、舞者等 (a professional entertainer, especially a singer or dancer)
COBUILD: An artiste is a professional entertainer, such as a singer or dancer, especially one who performs on stage.
搭配: cabaret artiste; recording artiste 歌舞表演艺人；录音歌手
例句: She began her career as a cabaret artiste.
她以歌舞表演艺人的身份开始了自己的职业生涯。
🏛️ 来源历史:
直接借自法语 artiste，与英语 artist 同源，最终都与拉丁语 ars（艺术、技艺）有关。英语中的法语形式逐渐专用于表演艺人，常带有正式或略显老派的语气。
Borrowed directly from French artiste, it shares its ancestry with English artist and ultimately relates to Latin ars, meaning 'art' or 'skill'. In English, the French form became associated particularly with entertainers and often has a formal or slightly old-fashioned tone.
🧩 构词衍生:
artiste 在现代英语中作为完整借词使用；art + -iste 是对其法语结构的分析，-iste 表示从事某种活动的人。复数为 artistes；相关词族有 art（艺术）、artist（艺术家）、artistic（艺术的）、artistically（艺术地）和 artistry（艺术技巧），但这些词并非由英语 artiste 逐一派生。
Artiste functions as a complete loanword in modern English; art + -iste describes its French structure, with -iste identifying someone engaged in an activity. Its plural is artistes; the related family includes art, artist, artistic, artistically, and artistry, though these are not successive English derivatives of artiste.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
performer（表演者）范围最广，不一定是职业人士；entertainer（演艺人员）强调给观众带来娱乐；artist（艺术家）范围更广，可指视觉艺术家，也可指音乐家或其他表演艺术家。
Performer is broad and does not necessarily imply professional status; entertainer emphasizes providing enjoyment for an audience; artist has a wider artistic range, covering visual artists as well as musicians and other performing artists.
💡 秒记锚点:
把 artist 后的法语式词尾 -e 当作提示：聚光灯下的表演艺人。
Use the French-looking final -e as a cue: picture an artist performing under stage lights.
convivial 1 词性 · 双语对齐
GRECEFR-C2
adj. 形容词 //kənˈvɪviəl// 欢乐友好的；气氛融洽的；好交际的 (friendly and cheerful, especially in social company; enjoying social gatherings)
COBUILD: A convivial person enjoys spending time with other people, and a convivial occasion or atmosphere is friendly, relaxed, and cheerful.
搭配: a convivial atmosphere; a convivial evening 欢乐融洽的气氛；欢乐愉快的夜晚
例句: The shared meal created a convivial atmosphere.
大家一起用餐，营造出了欢乐融洽的气氛。
🏛️ 来源历史:
来自拉丁语 convivialis（宴饮的），源于 convivium（宴会），其更深层来源与 con-（共同）和 vivere（生活）有关。词义由共同宴饮扩展为聚会中的欢乐友好，也用于形容喜欢交际的人。
It comes from Latin convivialis, meaning 'relating to a feast', from convivium, a banquet, ultimately associated with con- meaning 'together' and vivere meaning 'to live'. Its meaning broadened from feasting together to cheerful sociability and to people who enjoy it.
🧩 构词衍生:
现代英语通常把 convivial 作为整体形容词使用；从历史结构看可分析为 convivi-（宴饮）+ -al（形容词后缀），不宜把 vivial 当作独立英语词。主要派生词为 convivial + -ity → conviviality（欢乐友好的气氛；好交际）和 convivial + -ly → convivially（欢乐友好地）。
Modern English normally treats convivial as a whole adjective; historically, it can be analyzed as convivi-, referring to feasting, + adjectival -al, but vivial is not an independent English word. Its principal derivatives are convivial + -ity → conviviality, meaning cheerful sociability, and convivial + -ly → convivially.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
sociable（好交际的）强调愿意与人来往；jovial（快活友善的）强调外露的愉快性情；festive（喜庆的）强调庆祝氛围，而 convivial 特别突出相聚时的友好欢乐。
Sociable emphasizes willingness to mix with others; jovial emphasizes an openly cheerful temperament; festive emphasizes celebration, while convivial particularly suggests friendly enjoyment in company.
💡 秒记锚点:
从“共同生活”想到“同桌宴饮”：大家相聚，自然欢乐融洽。
Move from 'living together' to 'feasting together': shared company suggests friendly, cheerful enjoyment.
