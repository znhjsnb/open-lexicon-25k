available 3 个词性 · 全双语对照

adj. 形容词 //əˈveɪləbəl// 可获得的；可使用的；可供购买的 (obtainable or ready for use or purchase)
English: If something is available, you can obtain it, use it, or buy it.
搭配： readily available; available online 容易获得的；可在网上获取或购买的
例句： The report is available online in both English and Chinese.
这份报告的英文版和中文版均可在网上获取。
adj. 形容词 //əˈveɪləbəl// 有空的；可以提供帮助或参与的 (free to help, meet, or take part)
English: If someone is available, they are not too busy to meet you, help you, or do something.
搭配： available for work; available to help 可以工作的；有空提供帮助的
例句： Are you available for a short meeting tomorrow afternoon?
你明天下午有空开个短会吗？
adj. 形容词 //əˈveɪləbəl// 没有恋爱对象的；可以交往的 (not in a romantic relationship and open to dating)
English: If someone is available in a romantic sense, they are not already in a relationship and may be interested in dating.
搭配： single and available; an available man 单身且愿意交往的；可以交往的单身男子
例句： She asked whether he was single and available.
她问他是否单身、可以交往。
🏛️ 来源历史 (Etymology):
available 形成于晚期中古英语，由 avail 与形容词后缀 -able 构成；avail 经古法语表达追溯至拉丁语 valere，意为“强健；有价值”。其早期意义侧重“有效的；有用的”，后来发展出“可利用的；可获得的”，并扩展用于表示人有空。
Available arose in late Middle English from avail and the adjective suffix -able; avail ultimately traces through Old French expressions to Latin valere, meaning 'to be strong or worth something.' Earlier meanings concerned effectiveness or usefulness, later developing into accessibility for use and a person's freedom to participate.
🧩 构词衍生 (Morphology):
构词联系：avail（有用；帮助）+ -able（形容词后缀）→ available，但现代“可获得的”义需整体掌握。常用词族：availability（可获得性；可用性）、unavailable（无法获得的；没空的）、unavailability（无法获得；无暇）；相关词 avail 可作动词或名词，常见于 avail oneself of（利用）和 to no avail（徒劳）。available 不表示“免费”。
Morphological relationship: avail ('be useful; help') + -able (adjective suffix) → available, whose modern meaning should also be learned as a whole. The common word family includes availability, unavailable, and unavailability; related avail occurs as a verb or noun, especially in avail oneself of and to no avail. Available does not mean 'free of charge.'
⚖️ 近义微雕 (Nuances):
accessible（可接近或获取的）强调能否接触、进入或取得；obtainable（可得到的）强调是否能够获得；free（有空的）用于人时强调没有事务占用，而 available 更强调可以参与某项活动或提供帮助。
Accessible emphasizes the ability to reach, enter, or access something; obtainable emphasizes the possibility of acquiring it; free, when describing a person, emphasizes having no competing commitments, while available often highlights readiness for a particular activity or request.
💡 秒记锚点 (Memory Hook):
围绕“可供使用”记：东西可取得，人可腾出时间；可用不等于免费。
Think 'ready for use': a thing can be obtained, and a person can spare the time; availability says nothing about price.
borrow 3 个词性 · 全双语对照

v. 动词 //ˈbɒrəʊ// 借入；暂借；借用 (take and use something temporarily with the intention of returning it)
English: If you borrow something from someone, you use it for a period of time and intend to return it.
搭配： borrow a book; borrow something from someone 借一本书；向某人借某物
例句： Can I borrow your dictionary until Friday?
我能借用你的词典到星期五吗？
v. 动词 //ˈbɒrəʊ// 借款；借贷 (receive money that must be repaid)
English: If you borrow money, someone gives it to you on the understanding that you will pay it back.
搭配： borrow money; borrow from a bank 借钱；向银行借款
例句： They borrowed money from a bank to start their business.
他们向银行借钱创业。
v. 动词 //ˈbɒrəʊ// 借鉴；采用；借用词语或创意 (adopt an idea, word, or feature from another source)
English: If you borrow an idea, word, or style, you take it from another source and use it for your own purposes.
搭配： borrow an idea; borrow words from another language 借鉴一个想法；从另一种语言借用词语
例句： The designer borrowed ideas from traditional Chinese gardens.
这位设计师借鉴了中国传统园林的设计理念。
🏛️ 来源历史 (Etymology):
borrow 经中古英语 borwen 追溯至古英语 borgian，与表示“担保；抵押物”的 borg 有历史联系。其借入或借出的早期用法后来收窄为现代主要的“借入”，并扩展为借鉴创意、借用词语。
Borrow comes through Middle English borwen from Old English borgian, historically related to borg, meaning a pledge or security. Earlier uses covered both borrowing and lending; modern English mainly retains the receiving side and extends it to adopting ideas and words.
🧩 构词衍生 (Morphology):
borrow 是不可再按现代英语词义拆分的词基，不能拆成 bor + row。常用词族：borrower（借用者；借款人）、borrowing（借用；借款；借词）、borrowings（借款项目或总额；借入的词语或事物）；动词变化为 borrows、borrowed、borrowing，borrowed 也可作分词形容词，表示“借来的；借用的”。
Borrow is a base word with no useful smaller meaning-bearing parts in modern English; it is not bor + row. Common family members include borrower and the noun borrowing, whose plural borrowings can refer to borrowed funds, words, or other adopted elements. Verb forms are borrows, borrowed, and borrowing; borrowed also functions as a participial adjective.
⚖️ 近义微雕 (Nuances):
take out a loan（贷款）专指取得需要偿还的资金；adopt（采用）强调接受并使用某种方法或观念，不必突出来源；draw on（利用；借鉴）强调从知识、经验或创意中获取资源。borrow 的基本方向是“借入”，与表示“借出”的 lend 相对。
Take out a loan specifically means obtaining repayable funds; adopt emphasizes accepting and using a method or idea without necessarily highlighting its source; draw on emphasizes using knowledge, experience, or ideas as resources. Borrow describes receiving something temporarily, whereas lend describes providing it.
💡 秒记锚点 (Memory Hook):
方向锚点：borrow 是“借进来”，lend 是“借出去”；借创意时，保留“从别处取来使用”的逻辑。
Anchor the direction: borrow brings something in, while lend sends it out; borrowing an idea keeps the logic of taking something from another source to use.
budget 4 个词性 · 全双语对照

n. 名词 //ˈbʌdʒɪt// 预算；预算额；可支配的资金或资源 (a spending plan or an amount of money or resources available for a purpose)
English: A budget is a plan showing how much money you expect to receive and spend, or the amount of money or other resources available for something.
搭配： a tight budget; within budget 紧张的预算；在预算范围内
例句： We have a limited budget for the project, so we need to control costs.
这个项目的预算有限，因此我们需要控制成本。
v. 动词 //ˈbʌdʒɪt// 编制预算；安排开支；为某事预留资金 (plan spending or set aside money for a purpose)
English: If you budget, you plan how to spend your money; if you budget a particular amount for something, you include that amount in your spending plan.
搭配： budget carefully; budget for repairs 精打细算地安排开支；为维修预留预算
例句： We budgeted £500 for repairs this year.
我们今年安排了500英镑的维修预算。
v. 动词 //ˈbʌdʒɪt// 规划或分配时间、精力等资源 (plan and allocate time, effort, or other limited resources)
English: If you budget your time or energy, you decide how much to use for each activity so that you have enough for everything you need to do.
搭配： budget your time; budget time for revision 合理安排时间；为复习预留时间
例句： You need to budget your time carefully during the exam.
考试时你需要仔细安排时间。
adj. 形容词 //ˈbʌdʒɪt// 价格低廉的；经济型的 (inexpensive and intended for people who want to spend less)
English: Budget goods or services are offered at relatively low prices for people who want to limit their spending.
搭配： a budget hotel; a budget airline 经济型酒店；廉价航空公司
例句： We stayed in a budget hotel near the station.
我们住在车站附近的一家经济型酒店里。
🏛️ 来源历史 (Etymology):
budget 经中古英语 bowgette 来自法语 bougette，原指“小皮袋”，是 bouge（皮袋）的指小形式。后来它与财政文件袋及袋内的收支计划联系起来，逐渐发展为“预算”，再衍生出动词“安排预算”和形容词“经济型的”。
Budget comes through Middle English bowgette from French bougette, meaning a small leather bag and formed as a diminutive of bouge. Its association with a bag containing financial papers developed into the meaning of a financial plan, followed by the verb meaning 'plan expenditure' and the adjective meaning 'inexpensive.'
🧩 构词衍生 (Morphology):
budget 在现代英语中是完整词基，不能拆成 bud + get；名词、动词和形容词之间存在零派生关系。常用词族：budgetary（预算的）、budgeting（预算编制；动词 -ing 形式）、budgeter（编制或管理预算的人）；变化形式有 budgets、budgeted。相关构词包括 overbudget（预算编得过高）、underbudget（预算编得不足）、budget-friendly（价格实惠的）、low-budget（预算低的）、big-budget（投入巨大的）；over budget 和 under budget 分别表示“超出预算”和“低于预算”。
Budget is a complete modern base, not bud + get; its noun, verb, and adjective uses are related through conversion without an added suffix. Common family members include budgetary, budgeting, and budgeter, with inflected forms budgets and budgeted. Related formations include overbudget ('set a budget too high'), underbudget ('allow too little'), budget-friendly, low-budget, and big-budget; the phrases over budget and under budget describe spending above or below the planned amount.
⚖️ 近义微雕 (Nuances):
spending plan（开支计划）最接近预算名词，强调预计如何花钱；allocate（分配）与预算动词相近，但强调具体划拨资源，而 budget 强调整体规划及限额；inexpensive（不贵的）与形容词 budget 相近，前者只描述价格，后者常指面向节省开支者的产品或服务类别。
Spending plan closely matches the noun and emphasizes intended expenditure; allocate overlaps with the verb but focuses on assigning particular resources, while budget emphasizes planning within limits; inexpensive describes a low price, whereas adjectival budget often identifies a category of goods or services aimed at economical spending.
💡 秒记锚点 (Memory Hook):
从“小钱袋”串联：袋里有多少是预算，把钱怎么分是做预算，少花钱的选择是经济型。
Picture a small money bag: its limits suggest a budget, dividing its contents suggests budgeting, and spending less suggests a budget option.
commute 4 个词性 · 全双语对照

v. 动词（不及物） //kəˈmjuːt// 通勤；定期往返于住所与工作或学习地点 (to travel regularly between home and work or a place of study)
English: If you commute, you regularly travel between your home and the place where you work or study.
搭配： commute to work; commute by train 通勤上班；乘火车通勤
例句： She commutes to work by train every weekday.
她每个工作日都乘火车通勤上班。
n. 名词（可数） //kəˈmjuːt// 通勤路程；上下班或上下学的往返行程 (a regular journey between home and work or a place of study)
English: Your commute is the journey you regularly make between your home and the place where you work or study.
搭配： a daily commute; a long commute 每日通勤；漫长的通勤路程
例句： My daily commute takes about forty minutes each way.
我每天通勤，单程大约需要四十分钟。
v. 动词（及物，法律用语） //kəˈmjuːt// 减刑；将刑罚改判为较轻的刑罚 (to replace a punishment with a less severe one)
English: If an authority commutes a sentence, it officially replaces that punishment with a less severe one.
搭配： commute a sentence; commute a death sentence to life imprisonment 减刑；将死刑减为终身监禁
例句： The governor commuted his death sentence to life imprisonment.
州长将他的死刑减为终身监禁。
v. 动词（及物，财务用语） //kəˈmjuːt// 折算支付；将定期付款或养老金权益折为一次性付款 (to exchange regular payments or pension rights for a single payment)
English: If you commute a pension or other regular payments, you exchange all or part of them for a single payment.
搭配： commute a pension; commute pension benefits into a lump sum 将养老金折为一次性付款；将养老金权益折算为一笔款项
例句： She chose to commute part of her pension into a lump sum.
她选择将部分养老金折算为一次性付款。
🏛️ 来源历史 (Etymology):
commute 源自拉丁语 commūtāre，意为“改变、交换”，由 com-（共同、一起）与 mūtāre（改变）构成，英语中的减刑和折算支付义保留了“以一种形式替换另一种形式”的线索。通勤义经由美国铁路的 commutation ticket（以优惠价格供定期乘车使用的车票）发展而来，随后用于指持此类车票定期往返，不能仅凭拉丁语词根直接推出。
Commute comes from Latin commūtāre, meaning 'to change or exchange', formed from com- ('together') and mūtāre ('to change'); its punishment and payment senses preserve the idea of replacing one form with another. The travel sense developed through American railway commutation tickets, which offered reduced fares for regular travel, and subsequently came to describe the regular journeys themselves.
🧩 构词衍生 (Morphology):
现代英语中 commute 通常作为完整词干使用，不宜拆成 com- + mute，更与 mute“静音”无关；动词 commute 可零派生为名词 commute。主要词族：commute + -er → commuter（通勤者，直接加 r，保留原有 e）；commute 去 e + -ing → commuting（通勤）；commute + -d → commuted（通勤、减刑或折算的过去式及过去分词）；commute 去 e + -able → commutable（可通勤到达的；可减刑或折算的），以及 commutability（可折算性等）；commutation（减刑；折算支付，采用 commut- 词干）。复合派生包括 telecommute（远程办公）、telecommuter（远程办公者）、telecommuting（远程办公），以及 noncommutable（不可减刑或折算的）、uncommuted（未减刑或未折算的）。共享历史词根的专业词族还包括 commutative（满足交换律的）、commutativity（交换性）、noncommutative（不满足交换律的）、noncommutativity（非交换性）和 commutator（换向器；数学中的交换子），不应将这些专业含义混入日常通勤义。
In modern English, commute is normally treated as a whole stem, not as com- + mute, and it is unrelated to mute meaning 'silent'; the verb also forms the noun commute by conversion. The principal family includes commute + -er → commuter (add r directly, retaining the existing e); commute minus e + -ing → commuting; commute + -d → commuted; commute minus e + -able → commutable ('within commuting reach' or 'capable of being commuted'), with commutability; and commutation ('sentence reduction' or 'conversion of payments'), using the stem commut-. Further formations include telecommute, telecommuter, telecommuting, noncommutable, and uncommuted. Specialized relatives sharing the historical root include commutative, commutativity, noncommutative, noncommutativity, and commutator; their mathematical or electrical meanings are distinct from everyday commuting.
⚖️ 近义微雕 (Nuances):
travel（旅行、出行）范围最广，commute 特指住所与工作或学习地点之间的规律性往返；reduce（减少、减轻）用于刑罚时强调程度或期限降低，commute 强调由有权机关正式改为较轻刑罚；convert（转换）适用于多种形式变化，commute 在财务语境中特指将定期付款等折为另一种支付形式，通常为一次性付款。
Travel covers journeys generally, whereas commute specifically describes regular journeys between home and work or study. Reduce emphasizes lowering a punishment's severity or duration, whereas commute emphasizes its official replacement with a less severe punishment. Convert describes many changes of form, whereas financial commute specifically concerns exchanging payment arrangements, usually regular payments for a lump sum.
💡 秒记锚点 (Memory Hook):
先记“替换”：重刑换轻刑，定期付款换一次付款；再接历史桥梁“优惠定期车票 → 日常往返 → 通勤”。
Start with replacement: a lighter sentence replaces a harsher one, and a lump sum replaces regular payments; then remember the historical bridge: reduced-fare regular-travel ticket → repeated journeys → commuting.
