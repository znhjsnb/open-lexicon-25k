🏛️ 来源历史:

Matron came through Old French matrone from Latin matrona, meaning a married woman or wife, and is related to mater, meaning mother. Its meaning extended from a mature, respected woman to a woman responsible for supervising or caring for others in institutions.

In modern English, matron is a complete lexical base rather than a productive combination of mat and -ron. Its common family includes matrons, the plural; matronly, meaning mature and respectable in a way associated with a middle-aged woman, sometimes implying an older or conservative appearance; and matronliness, the corresponding quality.

Surname comes from Anglo-French surnom, formed from sur-, meaning above or additional, and nom, meaning name; its English form was influenced by name. It originally referred to an additional name or designation and later chiefly meant a hereditary family name.
🧩 构词衍生:
现代英语中 surname 通常作为完整词基使用；历史结构可理解为 sur-（额外）+ name（名字），但不表示姓氏必须写在名字上方或后面。词形包括 surnames（名词复数或动词第三人称单数）、surnamed（动词过去式和过去分词）、surnaming（动词现在分词）。
Modern English normally treats surname as a complete lexical base. Its historical structure can be understood as sur-, meaning additional, plus name, without implying any required written position. Its forms are surnames, the plural noun or third-person singular verb; surnamed, the past tense and past participle; and surnaming, the present participle.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
family name（家族姓氏）强调家庭传承，且不预设姓名顺序；last name（姓）常用于英语日常表达，但字面上的“最后一个名字”不适用于所有文化的姓名顺序；byname（别名、附加称号）通常指额外称呼，不一定是世袭姓氏。
Family name emphasizes family identity without assuming name order. Last name is an everyday equivalent in English, although family names do not come last in every naming tradition. Byname means an additional name or descriptive designation and need not be hereditary.

n. 名词 //ɪmˈbæŋkmənt// 堤岸；用于挡水或承托公路、铁路的路堤 (a raised bank of earth or stone built to hold back water or support a road or railway)
COBUILD: An embankment is a raised structure of earth or stone built to prevent flooding or to carry a road or railway above the surrounding ground.
搭配: railway embankment; river embankment 铁路路堤；河堤
例句: Heavy rain washed away part of the railway embankment.
暴雨冲毁了部分铁路路堤。
🏛️ 来源历史:
embankment 由动词 embank“用堤围住、筑堤防护”加名词后缀 -ment 构成；embank 又由 em- 和 bank“土堤、岸”组成。这个表示筑堤及其结果的词，后来广泛用于河道防护设施以及承托公路、铁路的高起土石结构。
Embankment was formed from embank, meaning to enclose or protect with a bank, and the noun suffix -ment; embank itself combines em- with bank in its earth-bank sense. The word came to describe raised structures used both for water control and for supporting roads and railways.
🧩 构词衍生:
构词公式：em-（en- 在 b 前的变体，此处表示围护或设置）+ bank（土堤）→ embank（筑堤围护）；embank + -ment → embankment（堤岸、路堤）。主要词族与词形：embank、embanks、embanked、embanking；embankment、embankments。这里的 bank 是“土堤、岸”，不是“银行”。
Formation: em-, a form of en- used before b and here expressing enclosure or provision, + bank, meaning an earth bank, produces embank; embank + -ment produces embankment. The principal family and inflected forms are embank, embanks, embanked, embanking, embankment, and embankments. Bank here refers to raised ground, not a financial institution.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
bank（土岸、土堤）含义更宽，可天然形成，也可人工修筑；dyke/dike（堤坝）通常强调挡水或防洪；levee（防洪堤）尤其指沿河修建的防洪设施。embankment 还可以承托道路或铁路，因此不一定承担挡水功能。
Bank is broader and can describe natural or artificial raised ground. Dyke, also spelled dike, usually emphasizes a barrier against water. Levee especially refers to a flood barrier alongside a river. An embankment may instead support a road or railway, so water control is not essential to its meaning.
💡 秒记锚点:
把土石筑成高起的 bank：挡水就是堤岸，托住道路就是路堤。
Build up a bank of earth: it can hold water back or lift a road above the ground.

Tummy arose as a nursery alteration of stomach, with the familiar or diminutive ending -y. It developed into an affectionate everyday term for the stomach or belly, especially when speaking to children.
🧩 构词衍生:
现代可联系口语词 tum（肚子）+ 亲昵后缀 -y → tummy，拼写时双写 m；它与 stomach 的关系属于缩略变形，不是规则截取。复数为 tummy 去 y 加 -ies → tummies。常见相关复合表达：tummy ache/tummyache（肚子痛）、tummy bug（肠胃感染）、tummy tuck（腹部整形术）。
A useful modern analysis is tum, an informal word for the stomach, + the affectionate suffix -y → tummy, with the m doubled. Its relationship to stomach involves an altered shortening rather than regular clipping. The plural is tummies, replacing y with -ies. Common related compounds include tummy ache or tummyache, tummy bug, and tummy tuck.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
belly（肚子）口语化，常指腹部外形或整个肚腹；stomach（胃、肚子）既可准确指消化器官，也可日常泛指腹部；abdomen（腹部）较正式，常用于解剖或医疗语境。tummy 最突出亲切、儿童化的语气。
Belly is informal and often refers to the visible abdominal area. Stomach can mean the digestive organ specifically or the abdominal area in everyday speech. Abdomen is more formal and common in anatomical or medical contexts. Tummy has the strongest affectionate or child-oriented tone.
💡 秒记锚点:

Picture a child patting a full belly: tummy is the friendly, childlike word for that stomach area.
