🧩 构词衍生:

In modern English, make is a free root with no productive internal affix division. Its inflected forms are makes, made, and making; made serves as both past tense and past participle, and making drops the final silent e. The main family includes maker, making, makings, remake, and unmake, with common compounds including makeup or make-up, makeover, make-believe, and makeshift.
⚖️ 近义微雕:

Create emphasizes bringing something new or original into existence; produce emphasizes output, often in industrial or formal contexts; cause focuses on a causal relationship. Make is more general and can describe production, actions, or resulting states, but these alternatives cannot replace it in every fixed expression.

Time is a free root used as both noun and verb, with the verb formed by conversion. Its inflections are times, timed, and timing, with the final silent e dropped in timing. Duration focuses on how long something lasts; occasion refers to a particular event or instance; period emphasizes a stretch of time with some boundaries. Time is broader and can refer to time generally, a moment, a duration, or an occurrence.

🏛️ 来源历史:

🧩 构词衍生:
who 是不可作现代词根加词缀拆分的简单代词；其格形式包括主格 who、宾格 whom 和属格 whose。现代日常英语常用 who 代替宾格 whom，但正式表达中 whom 仍然常见，尤其用于介词之后；扩展形式有 whoever（无论谁）、whomever（无论谁，宾格）和强调疑问的 who-ever／who ever；who's 是 who is 或 who has 的缩写，不等于 whose。
Who is a simple pronoun without a productive root-plus-affix breakdown. Its case forms are subject who, object whom, and genitive whose. Everyday English often uses who in place of object whom, while whom remains common in formal constructions, especially after a preposition; extended forms include whoever, whomever, and emphatic who ever, sometimes written who-ever. Who's contracts who is or who has and is distinct from whose.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
which person（哪一个人）与疑问 who 接近，但更突出从某个范围中选择；the person who（……的那个人）可展开说明部分关系从句结构；that（关系代词）可在限定性关系从句中指人，但不能像 who 一样用于非限定性关系从句。who 没有能适用于所有语境的单词近义词。
Which person is close to interrogative who but more strongly suggests selection from a set; the person who expands some constructions referring to an identified person; relative that can refer to people in defining clauses but cannot replace who in non-defining clauses. Who has no single-word synonym that works in every context.
💡 秒记锚点:

Who points to a person: a question identifies the person, and a relative clause describes the person.

你到了以后请给我发条消息。
🏛️ 来源历史:
me 源自古英语 mē，是第一人称单数代词的宾格、与格形式，并可追溯至更早的印欧语代词形式。英语失去大量格变化后，me 仍作为 I 对应的宾格形式保留下来。
Me comes from Old English mē, an accusative and dative form of the first-person singular pronoun, with earlier Indo-European ancestry. As English lost much of its case system, me survived as the object form corresponding to I.
🧩 构词衍生:
me 是不可拆分的代词形式，不含可分析的前后缀，也没有普通名词式复数。所属代词范式为 I（主格）—me（宾格）—my（物主限定词）—mine（物主代词）—myself（反身代词）；对应的复数形式组是 we—us—our—ours—ourselves。这些主要是语法范式关系，不是由 me 加词缀产生的派生词。
Me is an indivisible pronoun form with no analyzable prefix or suffix and no regular noun-like plural. Its pronoun paradigm is I, me, my, mine, and myself; the corresponding plural paradigm is we, us, our, ours, and ourselves. These are primarily grammatical relationships rather than derivatives formed by adding affixes to me.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
I 与 me 指同一说话者，但 I 通常作主语，me 通常作宾语；myself 用于反身关系或强调，不能任意替代 me；the speaker 可在说明性文字中指说话者，但不具有 me 的直接第一人称视角。
I and me refer to the same speaker, but I normally serves as a subject and me as an object; myself expresses reflexivity or emphasis and cannot freely replace me; the speaker can identify the same person in explanatory prose but lacks me's direct first-person viewpoint.
💡 秒记锚点:
“我做”通常用 I，“对我做、给我、和我”用 me：I call; call me.
Use I for the speaker doing the action and me for its recipient or target: I call; call me.

adv. 副词 /UK /ˈɑːftəwəd/; US /ˈæftɚwɚd/（英式；美式）/ 后来；之后；随后 (at a later time, following the event mentioned)
COBUILD: If something happens afterward, it happens after the event or time you have just mentioned.
搭配: shortly afterward; immediately afterward 不久之后；紧接着
例句: We watched the film and discussed it afterward.
我们看了这部电影，之后进行了讨论。
🏛️ 来源历史:
afterward 可追溯至古英语 æfterweard，由表示“在后、随后”的成分和表示方向的 -weard 构成。它把“朝后、位于后面”的方向关系用于时间，发展为现代的“之后”。
Afterward goes back to Old English æfterweard, combining an element meaning after or behind with the directional element -weard. A relationship of position or direction came to express the modern temporal meaning of later.
🧩 构词衍生:
构词公式为 after（之后）+ -ward（朝某方向）。同义变体 afterwards 加有历史副词词尾 -s；afterward 在美式英语中较常见，afterwards 在英式英语中较常见，两者都正确。相关词有 after、later、thereafter 和 afterward(s)，其中 later 是意义相关词，不是同根派生词；afterword 意为“后记”，是不同的复合词。
The structure is after + the directional suffix -ward. The equivalent variant afterwards contains the historical adverbial ending -s; afterward is more usual in American English and afterwards in British English, though both are standard. Related vocabulary includes after, later, thereafter, and afterward(s); later is semantically related rather than a same-root derivative. Afterword, meaning a concluding section of a book, is a different compound.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
later 可相对现在或其他参照时间表示“稍后”，范围较宽；subsequently 更正式，突出事件的先后顺序；thereafter 指“在那之后”，常见于正式叙述，有时涵盖之后持续的一段时间。
Later can refer broadly to a time after now or another reference point; subsequently is more formal and highlights the sequence of events; thereafter means after that point and is common in formal narration, sometimes covering an ongoing period.
💡 秒记锚点:
after + -ward：沿时间线走到某事的后面，就是“之后”。
After + -ward: move along the timeline to the far side of an event, and you are afterward.
