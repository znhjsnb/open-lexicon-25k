n. 名词 //ˈəʊpiɔɪd// 阿片类物质；作用于阿片受体的天然、半合成或合成物质 (A natural, semisynthetic, or synthetic substance that acts on opioid receptors.)
COBUILD: An opioid is a substance that acts on particular receptors in the body and can produce effects such as pain relief.

COBUILD: An opioid substance or effect acts in a way associated with opium and related substances.

研究人员考察了阿片受体对这种物质的反应。
🏛️ 来源历史:
opioid 是20世纪由 opium（鸦片）的词干 opi- 与表示“类似……的”的 -oid 构成的词。它从“具有阿片样作用的”发展为一类作用于阿片受体的物质的名称，范围不限于直接来自鸦片的成分。
Opioid was formed in the twentieth century from opi-, based on opium, and -oid, meaning 'resembling.' The idea of having opium-like effects developed into a name for substances acting on opioid receptors, including substances not directly derived from opium.
🧩 构词衍生:
构词：opi-（来自 opium，鸦片）+ -oid（类似……的）→ opioid；名词与形容词同形。主要词族及相关形式：opioids（复数）、non-opioid（非阿片类的；非阿片类药物）、opioid-like（阿片样的）、opioidergic（涉及阿片类物质或其受体活动的，专业用语）。opium（鸦片）与 opiate（阿片制剂；阿片类的）是相关词，不是由 opioid 派生而来。
Formation: opi- (from opium) + -oid ('resembling') → opioid, used as both a noun and an adjective. Principal family members and related formations include opioids, non-opioid, opioid-like, and the specialist adjective opioidergic. Opium and opiate are related words, rather than derivatives of opioid.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
opiate（阿片制剂）传统上主要指直接源于鸦片的物质，通常比 opioid 范围窄；analgesic（镇痛药）按缓解疼痛的功能分类，包含许多非阿片类药物；narcotic（麻醉性药物、受管制毒品）在医学、法律和日常用语中的范围不同，不能与 opioid 始终互换。
Opiate traditionally refers mainly to substances directly derived from opium and is usually narrower than opioid. Analgesic classifies a drug by its pain-relieving function and includes many non-opioid drugs. Narcotic varies in meaning across medical, legal, and everyday contexts and is not consistently interchangeable with opioid.
💡 秒记锚点:

Connect opi- with opium and -oid with resemblance: an opium-like action gives the name to the substances producing it.

n. 名词 //mɑːk// 马赫数；物体速度与当地声速的比值 (the ratio of an object's speed to the local speed of sound)
COBUILD: A Mach number expresses how fast something moves compared with the speed of sound in the surrounding medium.
搭配: Mach number; fly at Mach 2 马赫数；以二倍当地声速飞行

这架飞机能以马赫数2飞行，即达到当地声速的两倍。
🏛️ 来源历史:
Mach 来自奥地利物理学家恩斯特·马赫的姓氏，用以纪念他对高速运动和激波的研究。这个名称后来用于表示速度与当地声速的比值，因此它不是一个处处对应相同实际速度的固定单位。
Mach comes from the surname of Austrian physicist Ernst Mach and honors his research into high-speed motion and shock waves. The name came to designate a ratio to the local speed of sound, so it does not represent one fixed physical speed under all conditions.
🧩 构词衍生:
现代英语中，Mach 是不可再作普通词根、词缀拆分的姓氏来源词。相关构词和组合有 Mach number（马赫数）、Mach + meter → machmeter（马赫表）、high-Mach（高马赫数的）和 low-Mach（低马赫数的）；Machian（马赫的；马赫学说的）由同一姓氏派生，但不是专指速度的词。
In modern English, Mach is a surname-based term with no ordinary root-and-affix breakdown. Related formations and combinations include Mach number, Mach + meter → machmeter, high-Mach, and low-Mach; Machian derives from the same surname but refers to Mach or his ideas rather than specifically to speed.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
Mach number（马赫数）是完整技术名称；speed（速度）表示运动快慢，本身不包含与声速的比较；airspeed（空速）表示相对于周围空气的速度，也不必以声速为基准。后两者都不是 Mach 的严格同义词。
Mach number is the full technical expression; speed describes how fast something moves without necessarily comparing it with sound; airspeed measures speed relative to the surrounding air. The latter two are related terms, not exact synonyms.
💡 秒记锚点:
把当地声速看作“1”：Mach 2 就是它的两倍。
Treat the local speed of sound as 1: Mach 2 means twice that speed.

sundry 源自古英语 syndrig“分开的、各自的”，与表示“分开”的 sundor 有关，经中古英语的相关形式演变为现代拼写。意义从“各自不同的”发展为“各种各样的”，复数名词 sundries 则表示归在一起的杂项物品。

In modern English, sundry is normally learned as a whole stem and should not be divided into sun + dry. The historical form sundriness is obsolete and is not a current common derivative.
⚖️ 近义微雕:

Various is the broadest term and emphasizes differences in kind; miscellaneous emphasizes items that do not fit one clear category; assorted often describes a mixed collection of goods or objects. Sundry is somewhat formal and commonly describes incidental items or expenses.
