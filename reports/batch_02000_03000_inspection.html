🏛️ 来源历史:

Possibility entered English through French possibilite and ultimately comes from Latin possibilitas, the state of being possible, related to possibilis. Its meaning expanded from the abstract capacity to happen or be true to particular options and opportunities for development.
🧩 构词衍生:
现代构词：possible（可能的）去掉词尾 -le，再加 -ility → possibility；也可将 -ible → -ibility 视为形容词转抽象名词的常见形式变化。复数：possibility 中的 y 改为 i，再加 -es → possibilities。主要词族：possible（可能的）、possibly（可能；也许）、possibility（可能性）、impossible（不可能的）、impossibly（不可能地；极其）、impossibility（不可能；不可能的事）、impossibilities（不可能的事，复数）。
Modern formation: possible loses final -le and takes -ility to form possibility; this can also be understood as the familiar adjective-to-noun alternation -ible → -ibility. The plural changes final y to i and adds -es: possibilities. The principal family includes possible, able to happen or be done; possibly, perhaps; possibility; impossible, unable to happen or be done; impossibly, in an impossible way or to an extreme degree; impossibility, the state of being impossible or an impossible thing; and its plural impossibilities.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
chance 通常更口语化，可指事情发生的概率，也可指机会；likelihood 强调发生的可能程度，常用于概率判断；potential 强调尚未实现的发展能力，对应 possibility 的“潜力”义，而不等于一般的发生可能性。
Chance is often more conversational and can mean either probability or an opportunity. Likelihood emphasizes how probable something is. Potential emphasizes an unrealized capacity for development and matches the developmental sense of possibility rather than its general probability sense.
💡 秒记锚点:
possible 是“可能的”，possibility 就是把“可能”变成可讨论的一种状态、选择或前景。
Possible says that something can happen; possibility turns that idea into a state, an option, or a future prospect.

Inspect focuses on the condition, quality, or compliance of something tangible. Investigate emphasizes discovering causes, facts, or background through a broader inquiry. Scrutinize means to examine with especially close and often critical attention.

🏛️ 来源历史:
legal 源自拉丁语 legalis，意为“法律的”，由 lex 的词干 leg- 加上形容词后缀构成，并经法语进入英语。它既可表示“与法律有关”，也可表示“法律所允许或认可”。
Legal comes from Latin legalis, meaning relating to law, formed from leg-, the stem of lex, law, with an adjective suffix, and entered English through French. It developed both the relational meaning connected with law and the meaning permitted or recognized by law.
🧩 构词衍生:
现代英语通常将 legal 作为完整形容词词基；其中 leg- 的“法律”含义属于拉丁词源分析，不是英语 leg（腿）。主要词族：legal + -ly → legally（在法律上；合法地，两个 l 均保留）；legal + -ity → legality（合法性）；il- + legal → illegal（非法的，il- 是否定前缀 in- 在 l 前的变体）；illegally（非法地）、illegality（违法；非法性）、legalize／legalise（使合法化）、legalization／legalisation（合法化）、legalism（墨守法律条文）、legalistic（拘泥法律条文的）、legalist（法律形式主义者）、legalese（晦涩的法律行话）、nonlegal／non-legal（与法律无关的）。职场名词 legal 来自对 legal department 或 legal staff 的省略。
Modern English usually treats legal as a whole adjective base; its historical leg- meaning law is unrelated to English leg, the body part. Principal derivatives are legal + -ly → legally, retaining both l's; legal + -ity → legality, lawfulness; il- + legal → illegal, with il- as the form of negative in- before l; illegally; illegality; legalize/legalise, to make lawful; legalization/legalisation; legalism, strict adherence to legal rules; legalistic, excessively concerned with legal technicalities; legalist; legalese, difficult legal language; and nonlegal/non-legal, unrelated to law. The workplace noun legal is shortened from legal department or legal staff.
⚖️ 近义微雕:

Lawful emphasizes conformity with law and often matches legal meaning permitted by law, but it does not generally replace legal meaning law-related. Legitimate emphasizes validity or justification under law, rules, tradition, or accepted standards. Permissible means allowed by a law, rule, or authority and is not restricted to legal contexts.
💡 秒记锚点:

Anchor both main meanings to law: legal advice concerns the law, and legal conduct is conduct the law allows.

🏛️ 来源历史:
recommend 经中古拉丁语 recommendare 和古法语 recommander 传入英语，其基础 commendare 有“托付、称许”之义，re- 在这里起加强作用。把人或事物以赞许的方式交托给别人考虑，逐渐发展为现代的“推荐、建议”。
Recommend reached English through Medieval Latin recommendare and Old French recommander, based on commendare, to entrust or praise, with an intensifying re-. The idea of presenting someone or something with approval developed into the modern meanings suggest as suitable and advise.
🧩 构词衍生:
现代通常把 recommend 作为整体动词词基；虽与 commend（称赞；推荐）相关，但这里的 re- 不宜按现代常见的“再次”来理解。屈折形式：recommends、recommended、recommending；主要派生：recommend + -ation → recommendation（推荐；建议）、recommend + -er → recommender（推荐者；推荐系统）、recommend + -able → recommendable（值得推荐的，较少用）、recommendatory（推荐性的，正式且较少用）、unrecommended（未被推荐的，较少用）；recommended 也可作形容词，表示“推荐的；建议的”。
Modern English normally treats recommend as a whole verb base. It is related to commend, meaning praise or present with approval, but re- here should not be interpreted as the usual modern again. Inflections are recommends, recommended, and recommending. Principal derivatives include recommend + -ation → recommendation, a suggestion or endorsement; recommend + -er → recommender, a person or system that recommends; recommend + -able → recommendable, worth recommending but less common; recommendatory, expressing a recommendation and formal or uncommon; and unrecommended, not recommended and less common. Recommended also functions as an adjective meaning advised or endorsed.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
suggest 只是提出想法或选项，通常比 recommend 的认可程度弱；advise 更强调给出有助于行动或决策的意见，常带有经验或专业判断；endorse 强调明确表示支持或认可，常用于公开支持产品、人物或主张。
Suggest presents an idea or option and usually expresses less approval than recommend. Advise emphasizes guidance about what someone should do, often based on experience or expertise. Endorse emphasizes explicit approval or support, often publicly, for a product, person, or proposal.
💡 秒记锚点:
把“称许”与“交给别人考虑”连起来：认可一个选择，就推荐它；认可一种做法，就建议采用。
Connect approval with presenting something for consideration: approve a choice and recommend it; approve an action and recommend doing it.
