In modern English, glace/glacé is normally treated as a whole loanword; its final e is not an English suffix. Its French source contains glac- (ice or freezing) + -é (a past-participle ending). 🏛️ 来源历史:

🧩 构词衍生:

Globul- (small sphere, corresponding to globule without its final silent e) + -in (a substance-forming suffix) → globulin; plural globulins. Related forms include globule, globular, and globularity. Major compounds include immunoglobulin (an immune-system protein), antiglobulin (an antibody against globulins), macroglobulin (a large globulin), and thyroglobulin (a thyroid protein), alongside alpha, beta, and gamma globulin.
⚖️ 近义微雕:

There is no exact synonym covering every sense. Protein is the broader category; immunoglobulin denotes globulins with antibody functions; albumin is a different protein class, traditionally distinguished partly by water solubility, and is not interchangeable with globulin.

hydrocephalus 1 词性 · 双语对齐
n. 名词 /英 /ˌhaɪdrəʊˈsefələs/；美 /ˌhaɪdroʊˈsefələs// 脑积水，脑室内脑脊液异常积聚的病症 (A condition involving an abnormal buildup of cerebrospinal fluid in the brain's ventricles)
COBUILD: Hydrocephalus is a condition in which too much cerebrospinal fluid collects in the spaces called ventricles inside the brain. [NCI](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/hydrocephalus)
搭配: congenital hydrocephalus; normal-pressure hydrocephalus 先天性脑积水；正常压力脑积水
例句: The scan showed hydrocephalus with enlarged ventricles.
扫描显示存在脑积水，并伴有脑室扩大。
🏛️ 来源历史:
经拉丁语医学词形传入英语，源头是由希腊语 hydōr（水）和 kephalē（头）构成的词，字面意象是“头部有水”。现代医学中的“水”具体指脑脊液，并非普通的水。
The word entered English through Latin medical terminology and ultimately combines Greek hydōr, meaning water, with kephalē, meaning head. Its historical water-in-the-head image refers in modern medicine to cerebrospinal fluid rather than ordinary water. [Collins](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/english/hydrocephalus)
🧩 构词衍生:
hydro-（水、液体）+ cephal-（头）+ -us（拉丁化名词词尾）→ hydrocephalus。相关词有 hydrocephaly（脑积水，异形词）、hydrocephalic（脑积水的）、hydrocephalous（患脑积水的，较少用）、hydrocephaloid（类似脑积水的，罕见）。本词通常用作不可数病名；hydrocephalic 是另一个词，不能据此把 hydrocephalus 标作形容词。
Hydro- (water or fluid) + cephal- (head) + -us (a Latinized noun ending) → hydrocephalus. Related forms include hydrocephaly (an alternative noun), hydrocephalic (relating to hydrocephalus), hydrocephalous (affected by hydrocephalus, less common), and hydrocephaloid (resembling hydrocephalus, rare). Hydrocephalus normally functions as an uncountable disease name; the separate adjective hydrocephalic does not give hydrocephalus an adjective use.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
hydrocephaly（脑积水）是较少用的同义形式；water on the brain（脑中积水）是非专业俗称；ventriculomegaly（脑室扩大）指影像学上的脑室增大，可有不同原因，不能一律等同于脑积水。
Hydrocephaly is a less common synonym; water on the brain is a nontechnical expression; ventriculomegaly describes enlarged ventricles on imaging and can have different causes, so it is not an exact synonym.
💡 秒记锚点:
hydro（水）+ cephal（头）：头内液体积多了；医学上记为脑室内脑脊液异常积聚。
Hydro means water and cephal means head: connect the name with excess cerebrospinal fluid in the brain's ventricles.

COBUILD: An irruption occurs when an animal population increases sharply or unusually large numbers of birds move into an area where they are normally less common.
搭配: a winter irruption; an irruption of snowy owls 冬季鸟类异常大量迁入；雪鸮的大规模异常迁入

那个冬天，观鸟者记录到雪鸮在整个地区异常大量出现。
🏛️ 来源历史:
来自拉丁语 irruptio，常经法语 irruption 传入；其相关动词 irrumpere 由表示“向内”的 in- 和 rumpere（打破）组成。最初“突破而入”的方向感，后来延伸到动物种群的突然增多和鸟类的大规模异常迁入。
The word comes from Latin irruptio, also transmitted through French irruption; the related verb irrumpere combines in-, meaning into, with rumpere, meaning break. The idea of bursting in later extended to sudden population increases and irregular mass arrivals of birds. [Merriam-Webster: irrupt](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/irrupt), [Merriam-Webster: irruption](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/irruption)
🧩 构词衍生:
现代词族关系可表示为 irrupt（闯入、数量骤增）+ -ion（行为或过程名词后缀）→ irruption；历史词根拆解为 ir-（in- 在 r 前的同化形式，向内）+ rupt（破裂）+ -ion。词族包括 irrupt、irrupts、irrupted、irrupting、irruption、irruptions、irruptive（突然侵入的；种群骤增的）；rupture（破裂）、eruption（喷发）、interruption（打断）是同根相关词。
The modern family relationship is irrupt (to rush in or increase suddenly) + -ion (an action or process noun suffix) → irruption. Historically, it contains ir- (in-, meaning into, assimilated before r) + rupt (break) + -ion. The family includes irrupt, irrupts, irrupted, irrupting, irruption, irruptions, and irruptive. Rupture, eruption, and interruption share the historical break root.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
incursion（侵入、袭扰）常指范围或时间有限的进入；influx（大量涌入）强调数量而不必猛烈；invasion（入侵）强调侵犯、占领或大规模侵入。irruption 尤其突出突然突破而入；其鸟类学义不能简单等同于规律性的 migration（迁徙）。
Incursion often suggests a limited intrusion; influx emphasizes large numbers arriving without necessarily implying force; invasion suggests intrusive entry, occupation, or overwhelming numbers. Irruption particularly stresses sudden bursting in, and its ornithological sense differs from regular migration.
💡 秒记锚点:
ir- 向内 + rupt 破：破门而入；对照 eruption 的向外喷发，再联想到鸟群突然大量涌来。
Ir- points inward and rupt means break: picture a door bursting inward, then a sudden flood of birds; compare eruption, which originally points outward.

这是一个现代科学术语，由源自希腊语 melas（黑色）的 melano- 与源自希腊语 kytos（中空容器，后用于表示细胞）的 -cyte 构成。名称以这种细胞制造黑色素的功能为依据。

🧩 构词衍生:
构词：melano-（黑色、黑色素）+ -cyte（细胞）。直接词族包括 melanocytes（复数）和 melanocytic（黑素细胞的）；相关同源词包括 melanin（黑色素）、melanogenesis（黑色素生成）和 melanoblast（成黑素细胞），这些不是由 melanocyte 直接派生而来。
Formation: melano- ('black; melanin') + -cyte ('cell'). Melanin-producing cell is an explanatory equivalent; pigment cell is broader; melanophore usually names a particular pigment cell in animals such as fish and amphibians and is not interchangeable with melanocyte in every context.
💡 秒记锚点:

Melano- points to melanin; -cyte means cell: a cell that makes melanin.

COBUILD: A piebald animal has a coat with large, irregular patches of black and white.
搭配: a piebald horse; a piebald pony 一匹黑白花斑马；一匹黑白花斑小马

n. 名词 //ˈpaɪbɔːld// 黑白花斑动物，尤指黑白花斑马 (a black-and-white animal, especially a horse)
COBUILD: A piebald is an animal, especially a horse, whose coat has black and white patches.
搭配: ride a piebald; a piebald gelding 骑黑白花斑马；一匹黑白花斑阉马

她选了那匹黑白花斑马来上骑术课。
🏛️ 来源历史:
该词形成于16世纪，由 pie（喜鹊的旧称）和 bald（当时可指带白斑的）组成，借用了喜鹊黑白相间的羽色。它后来也用作名词，指具有这种毛色的动物，尤其是马。
The word arose in the sixteenth century from pie, an older name for the magpie, and bald in a sense referring to white markings, evoking the bird's black-and-white plumage. It also became a noun for an animal, especially a horse, with that colouring.
🧩 构词衍生:
构词：pie（喜鹊的旧称）+ bald（历史义为带白斑的）；现代通常将其作为整体词使用。词族包括 piebalds（名词复数）、piebaldness（黑白花斑这一特征）和 piebaldism（斑驳病，一种先天性色素缺失状况）；医学词 piebaldism 不等同于普通的动物花斑毛色。
Formation: pie (an older word for 'magpie') + bald (historically referring to white markings); modern speakers generally treat it as a whole word. The family includes piebalds, piebaldness ('the quality of being piebald'), and piebaldism (a congenital condition involving patches lacking pigment); the medical term is distinct from ordinary animal coat colouring.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
pied（杂色的、花斑的）不限于黑白两色；skewbald（白色与黑色以外另一种颜色相间的）在马匹毛色中与 piebald 有明确区别；pinto（花斑马或花斑的）在美式用法中范围更广，可包括多种大片白斑毛色。
Pied means multicoloured or patched and is not limited to black and white; skewbald specifically describes white patches combined with a colour other than black in horses; pinto is broader in American usage and covers various coats with substantial white patches.
💡 秒记锚点:
想起喜鹊的黑白羽色：喜鹊配色的马，就是 piebald。
Picture a magpie's black-and-white plumage: a horse in magpie colours is piebald.
