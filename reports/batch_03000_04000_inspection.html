🏛️ 来源历史:

Calm entered Middle English through French and is generally connected with Italian calma, meaning stillness or an absence of wind, although its deeper origin is uncertain. Its meanings expanded from quiet weather or water to emotional composure and the action of making someone calm.
🧩 构词衍生:

In modern English, calm is a base word with no productive internal affix division and functions as an adjective, noun, and verb. Its family includes calm + -ly → calmly, retaining both l letters; calm + -ness → calmness; the comparative and superlative calmer and calmest; the verb forms calms, calmed, and calming, with calming also used as an adjective; and be- + calm → becalm, especially becalmed, meaning unable to sail because there is no wind.

Composed emphasizes self-control under pressure; peaceful emphasizes freedom from conflict or disturbance; soothe is a verb emphasizing gentle relief of distress or discomfort, while calm has a broader range of uses.

构词公式：un-（不）+ usual（通常的）→ unusual；unusual + -ly → unusually（异常地、不寻常地）；unusual + -ness → unusualness（不寻常性，较少见）。对应词族包括 usual（通常的）、usually（通常）、usualness（通常性，较少见）；un- 不改变 usual 的重音位置。
Formation: un- meaning not + usual → unusual; unusual + -ly → unusually; unusual + -ness → unusualness, a less common noun. The corresponding family includes usual, usually, and the less common noun usualness. Adding un- does not shift the stress of usual.
⚖️ 近义微雕:

Uncommon mainly emphasizes low frequency; strange often suggests unfamiliarity, puzzlement, or unease; distinctive emphasizes recognizable special features, while unusual need not express either approval or disapproval.
💡 秒记锚点:
un-＝不，usual＝通常：偏离“通常情况”，就是 unusual。
Un- means not and usual means normal: something outside the usual pattern is unusual.

💡 秒记锚点:

In modern English, plot is a base word functioning as both noun and verb. Storyline emphasizes the thread of a story, while plot emphasizes how its events are arranged and connected; conspiracy emphasizes secret cooperation between people for wrongdoing, while a plot may be devised by one person; parcel is common in property and legal contexts, while plot often highlights a piece of land's intended use.
💡 秒记锚点:

Use marking out and arranging as a memory link: mark out land or data points, arrange a route or story, and secretly arrange wrongdoing. This is a mnemonic, not a claim that every sense followed one proven historical path.

🧩 构词衍生:
现代英语通常以 deserve 为整体动词词基；不能把历史上的 de- 误解为现代常见的“去除、否定”。主要词族：deserves；deserve 去词尾 e + -ed → deserved（应得的）；去词尾 e + -ing → deserving（值得帮助、赞扬等的）；deservedly（理所当然地）、undeserved（不应得的）、undeservedly（不应当地）、undeserving（不配得到的）。惯用语 just deserts 中的 deserts 表示“应得的报应”，与 deserve 同源，不能写成表示“甜点”的 desserts。
Modern English normally treats deserve as a whole verb base; its historical de- should not be interpreted as the modern prefix meaning removal or reversal. Its main family includes deserves; deserved and deserving, formed by dropping final e before -ed or -ing; deservedly, undeserved, undeservedly, and undeserving. The related noun deserts means what someone deserves in the expression just deserts and must not be confused with desserts, meaning sweet dishes.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
merit 较正式，强调某事物的价值足以获得关注、表扬或处理；earn 强调通过努力或行为实际赢得，deserve 则表示理应得到，未必已经得到；warrant 常以情况、证据等作主语，表示足以构成采取某种行动或反应的理由。
Merit is more formal and emphasizes being worthy of attention, praise, or action; earn emphasizes obtaining something through effort or conduct, while deserve expresses what should be received even if it is not received; warrant usually describes a situation or evidence that provides sufficient reason for an action or response.
💡 秒记锚点:
从“尽心服务而挣得”记到“行为配得上结果”：好行为应得奖励，坏行为应受惩罚。
Move from earning through devoted service to conduct that merits an outcome: good conduct deserves rewards, and bad conduct may deserve punishment.
