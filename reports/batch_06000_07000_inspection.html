🧩 构词衍生:
现代构词关系为 local（地方的；当地的）+ -ity（性质或状态名词后缀）→ locality；复数变 y 为 i 加 -es，得到 localities。local 词族包括 locally（在当地；局部地）、localize/localise（使局限于某地；本地化）、localized/localised（局部的；本地化的）、localization/localisation（局部化；本地化）、localism（地方主义；地方用语）、localist（地方主义者；地方主义的）、nonlocal（非本地的）；locate（确定位置）和 location（位置）是共享历史词根的相关词。
The modern morphological relationship is local + the noun-forming suffix -ity → locality. The plural changes -y to -ies: localities. ⚖️ 近义微雕:

Area is the most general term and allows flexible size and boundaries. Vicinity specifically means the surroundings of a place. District often has administrative boundaries or a defining character, while locality is a relatively formal term for a particular place or area.

Authoritarian emphasizes obedience to authority and is broader than fascist. Totalitarian emphasizes comprehensive state control of society without necessarily identifying a fascist ideology. An autocrat holds absolute power or behaves domineeringly but need not support fascism.

COBUILD: A portable is a building that can be moved to another location and is often used as an additional classroom.

例句: The school added two portables to accommodate the extra students.

🏛️ 来源历史:
portable 经法语 portable 进入英语，源自拉丁语 portabilis，意为“可携带的”，其基础是 portare“携带”。它最初表示实物可以搬运，后来扩展到软件等能够在不同环境之间转移使用的事物。
Portable entered English through French portable, from Latin portabilis, meaning 'capable of being carried,' based on portare, 'to carry.' Its physical meaning later extended to things such as software that can be transferred between different environments.
🧩 构词衍生:
可按拉丁来源分析为 port-（携带）+ -able（能够被……的），但 portable 是整体借入英语的，并非由现代名词 port“港口”构成。主要词族有 portability（便携性；可移植性）、portably（以便携或可移植的方式）、nonportable / non-portable（不便携的；不可移植的）；名词复数为 portables（便携设备；活动建筑）。
Its Latin-based structure can be analyzed as port- (carry) + -able (capable of being), but portable was borrowed as a whole rather than formed from the modern noun port meaning a harbor. Principal family members are portability (ease of carrying or transferring), portably (in a portable manner), and nonportable / non-portable (not portable); the noun plural is portables.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
movable（可移动的）只说明能够挪动，不保证方便携带；mobile（可移动的；移动式的）强调移动能力或移动中使用；transportable（可运输的）强调能够运送，可能仍需要车辆或机械。portable 通常强调为方便携带或转移使用而设计。
Movable means something can be moved without implying convenient carrying; mobile emphasizes movement or use while moving; transportable means something can be transported, possibly with vehicles or machinery. Portable usually emphasizes convenient carrying or transfer for use elsewhere.
💡 秒记锚点:
port- 表示“携带”：设备能带走，代码能带到另一个系统。
Port- means 'carry': carry the device elsewhere, or carry the code onto another system.

Folly comes from Old French folie, 'foolishness,' related to fol, 'foolish'; the word history reaches back to Latin follis, 'bellows; leather bag,' which developed a disparaging application to a person as an empty bag. The idea of a foolish or wasteful undertaking later produced the architectural sense of an ornamental building with little practical purpose.
🧩 构词衍生:
现代英语中，folly 是整体词干，不宜机械拆成 fool + -y；复数为 folly → follies，把辅音字母后的 y 改为 ies。它与 fool（傻瓜；愚弄）、foolish（愚蠢的）、foolishly（愚蠢地）、foolishness（愚蠢）和 foolery（愚蠢的举动）同源，但这些不是直接在 folly 后添加词缀形成的。
In modern English, folly is a whole base and should not be mechanically divided into fool + -y; its plural is folly → follies, replacing consonant + y with consonant + ies. It is historically related to fool (a foolish person; to deceive), foolish, foolishly, foolishness, and foolery (foolish behavior), but these are not formed by adding suffixes directly to folly.
⚖️ 近义微雕:

Foolishness is the closest general equivalent and is relatively neutral; stupidity is more insulting and can suggest an inability to think properly; imprudence emphasizes insufficient attention to risks or consequences. Folly is more formal and often suggests avoidable errors of judgment with serious consequences.
💡 秒记锚点:

n. 可数名词 //ˈædʒɪktɪv// 形容词 (A word that describes or qualifies a noun or pronoun.)

搭配: a comparative adjective; an adjective phrase 比较级形容词；形容词短语
例句: In the phrase 'a quiet room,' 'quiet' is an adjective.
在短语 a quiet room（一个安静的房间）中，quiet 是形容词。
🏛️ 来源历史:
adjective 经法语进入英语，源自拉丁语语法术语 adjectivum，意为“附加的成分”，与 adicere“添加”有关。传统语法把形容词看作附加在名词上、说明其性质的词，由此形成了今天的词类名称。
Adjective entered English through French from the Latin grammatical term adjectivum, meaning 'something added,' related to adicere, 'to add.' Traditional grammar treated an adjective as a word added to a noun to describe its qualities, giving rise to the modern word-class name.
🧩 构词衍生:
现代英语中，adjective 通常作为完整词干学习；历史上可联系 ad-（向；加到）和表示“投掷”的拉丁语词根，但不宜把它当作现代英语中自由组合的构词公式。主要词族为 adjective → adjectives（复数），adjectival（形容词的；具有形容词功能的）→ adjectivally（作形容词地）；语言学中还有 adjectivize / adjectivise（使形容词化）和 adjectivization / adjectivisation（形容词化）。
In modern English, adjective is normally learned as a complete base; historically it contains elements associated with ad- (to; toward) and a Latin root meaning 'throw,' rather than a freely productive English construction. Principal family forms are adjective → adjectives, adjectival (relating to or functioning as an adjective) → adjectivally (in an adjectival function); technical linguistic forms include adjectivize / adjectivise (make into an adjective) and adjectivization / adjectivisation (that process).
⚖️ 近义微雕:
describing word（描述性词语）是教学中对 adjective 的通俗称呼，但不够严格；modifier（修饰语）范围更广，还包括副词、短语和从句；epithet（修饰性称号或描述语）强调用于刻画某人的词语，不一定是形容词。严格语法语境中，adjective 没有完全等义的常用单词替代。
Describing word is an informal teaching label for adjective but lacks precision; modifier is broader and includes adverbs, phrases, and clauses; epithet is a descriptive expression applied to someone and need not be an adjective. In precise grammatical usage, adjective has no exact everyday single-word substitute.
💡 秒记锚点:

n. 通常不可数名词；表示种类时可数 //mɒs// 藓；藓类植物；日常泛称苔藓 (Small nonflowering plants that often form soft green coverings on damp surfaces.)
COBUILD: Moss is a very small green plant without flowers that often grows in a soft layer on damp ground, rocks, or trees.
搭配: a carpet of moss; peat moss 一层地毯般的苔藓；泥炭藓；园艺用泥炭藓材料
例句: A thick carpet of moss covered the shaded rocks.
背阴的岩石上覆盖着一层厚厚的苔藓。
🏛️ 来源历史:
moss 来自古英语 mos，与其他日耳曼语言中表示藓类或沼泽的词同源。它的历史用法涉及藓类植被和湿地环境，现代常用义则主要指在潮湿表面形成绿色覆盖层的小型植物。
Moss comes from Old English mos and is related to words for moss or marshland in other Germanic languages. Its historical uses connect mossy vegetation with wet ground, while its usual modern meaning refers to small plants forming green coverings on damp surfaces.
🧩 构词衍生:
moss 是单纯词；表示整片苔藓时通常不可数，表示不同种类时可用 mosses。主要派生词为 moss + -y → mossy（长满苔藓的；像苔藓的），mossy → mossiness（多苔藓的状态）；另有 mossless（无苔藓的）、mosslike / moss-like（像苔藓的）和复合词 moss-covered（覆满苔藓的）、peat moss（泥炭藓；园艺泥炭藓材料）。
Moss is a simple base; it is usually uncountable for a covering of vegetation, but mosses can refer to different kinds. Principal derivatives are moss + -y → mossy (covered with or resembling moss) and mossy → mossiness (the condition of being mossy); further formations include mossless (without moss), mosslike / moss-like (resembling moss), moss-covered, and peat moss (a moss or associated material used in gardening).
⚖️ 近义微雕:
moss 没有准确的常用同义词：bryophyte（苔藓植物）是范围更广的植物学术语；lichen（地衣）是通常由真菌与藻类或蓝细菌形成的共生体；algae（藻类）则是另一类通常进行光合作用的生物。外观相似不代表三者可以互换。
Moss has no exact everyday synonym: bryophyte is a broader botanical term; lichen is a partnership usually involving a fungus and an alga or cyanobacterium; algae are a different group of generally photosynthetic organisms. Similar appearance does not make these terms interchangeable.
💡 秒记锚点:
湿石头上的绿色软地毯＝moss；铺满这种地毯就是 mossy。
Picture a soft green carpet on a damp rock: that is moss; a surface covered with it is mossy.
