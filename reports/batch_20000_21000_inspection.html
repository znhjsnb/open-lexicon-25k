n. 名词 //ˈkrɪbɪdʒ// 克里比奇纸牌游戏，通常用计分板和插钉记录分数 (a card game in which points are usually recorded with pegs on a board)
COBUILD: Cribbage is a card game in which players score points for particular combinations of cards and usually record their scores with pegs on a board.
搭配: play cribbage; a cribbage board 玩克里比奇纸牌；克里比奇牌戏计分板
例句: After dinner, they played cribbage and kept score on an old wooden board.
晚饭后，他们玩起了克里比奇纸牌，用一块旧木制计分板记录分数。
🏛️ 来源历史:
cribbage 在17世纪已有记载，由 crib 与后缀 -age 构成。在这种游戏中，crib 指双方各弃出部分牌后组成、由庄家额外计分的一手牌。
Recorded in the seventeenth century, cribbage was formed from crib and the suffix -age. In the game, the crib is an extra hand assembled from cards discarded by the players and scored by the dealer.
🧩 构词衍生:
现代构词：crib（游戏中的额外一手牌）+ -age（构成名词）→ cribbage，拼写中双写 b。cribbage 通常为不可数名词，没有常用的进一步派生词；相关表达有 cribbage board（计分板）和 cribbage player（玩家），其中 cribbage 作名词定语，并非独立的形容词词性。
Modern formation: crib, the extra hand in the game, + the noun-forming suffix -age → cribbage, with a doubled b. Cribbage is normally uncountable and has no common further derivatives; related expressions include cribbage board and cribbage player, where cribbage is an attributive noun rather than a separate adjective.
⚖️ 近义微雕:

There is no standard exact synonym. Card game is the broader category; whist and bridge are different card games organized mainly around winning tricks and cannot replace cribbage.
💡 秒记锚点:

dactyl 1 词性 · 双语对齐

n. 名词 //ˈdæktɪl// 扬抑抑格音步；古典格律中由一长两短音节组成的音步 (a metrical foot consisting of one stressed syllable followed by two unstressed syllables, or one long followed by two short syllables)
COBUILD: A dactyl is a group of three syllables in poetry, with the first stressed and the next two unstressed, or, in classical verse, the first long and the next two short.
搭配: a metrical dactyl; a line of dactyls 一个扬抑抑格音步；一行由扬抑抑格音步组成的诗
例句: The word 'merrily' can illustrate a dactyl because its first syllable is stressed and the next two are unstressed.
merrily 一词可以用来说明扬抑抑格，因为它的第一个音节重读，后两个音节轻读。
🏛️ 来源历史:
该词经拉丁语 dactylus 源自希腊语 daktylos，原义为“手指”。古人把手指一长两短的三节比作古典格律中一长两短的三个音节，英语重音诗律又用它表示一重两轻的音步。
The word came through Latin dactylus from Greek daktylos, meaning 'finger.' A finger's three sections, one long and two short, supplied an image for the classical long–short–short foot, later adapted to stressed–unstressed–unstressed rhythm in English.
🧩 构词衍生:
现代英语中通常把 dactyl 作为完整词基，不再拆分。词族：dactyls（复数音步）、dactylic（扬抑抑格的）、dactylically（以扬抑抑格方式）；dactyl-／dactylo- 在其他学术词中表示“手指、足趾”，与本词共享希腊语来源。
In modern English, dactyl is normally treated as a complete base rather than divided further. Its family includes dactyls, dactylic, and dactylically; the learned combining forms dactyl- and dactylo-, meaning 'finger' or 'toe,' share its Greek origin.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
没有完全等义的常用单词。metrical foot（音步）是上位词；anapest（抑抑扬格）为两轻一重，与 dactyl 次序相反；trochee（扬抑格）只有一重一轻两个音节。
There is no common exact single-word synonym. Metrical foot is the broader term; an anapest reverses the pattern to unstressed–unstressed–stressed, while a trochee has only two syllables, stressed–unstressed.
💡 秒记锚点:
手指三节：长—短—短；诗歌节奏：重—轻—轻。
Picture three finger sections: long–short–short, matching the poetic beat strong–weak–weak.

COBUILD: A galette is a flat, usually round French cake or pastry, often made with dough folded around the edge of a sweet or savory filling.
搭配: apple galette; galette des rois 苹果法式馅饼；法式国王饼
例句: She folded the pastry edges over the apples to make a galette.
她把酥皮边缘向内折，盖住部分苹果，做成了一只法式馅饼。
n. 名词 //ɡəˈlet// （法国布列塔尼式、通常配咸馅的）荞麦薄饼 (A Breton buckwheat pancake, usually served with savory fillings.)
COBUILD: A galette is also a thin buckwheat pancake from Brittany that is usually served with savory fillings.
搭配: buckwheat galette; ham and cheese galette 荞麦薄饼；火腿奶酪荞麦薄饼
例句: He ordered a buckwheat galette filled with ham, cheese, and an egg.
他点了一份夹有火腿、奶酪和鸡蛋的荞麦薄饼。
🏛️ 来源历史:
英语从法语 galette 借入该词；其名称与 galet（卵石、圆石）有关，通常认为借用了圆而扁的形象。法语中的名称用于多种扁圆饼，英语也用它指法式馅饼和布列塔尼荞麦薄饼。
English borrowed galette from French, where the name is related to galet, a pebble, generally understood as referring to a rounded, flattened shape. The French name covers several kinds of flat cakes, and English also uses it for French pastries and Breton buckwheat pancakes.
🧩 构词衍生:
现代英语中 galette 是完整借入词，不作能产性的英语词根加词缀拆分；复数为 galettes。法语词源上可联系 galet（卵石）及 -ette 形式，但这不是英语中的派生规则；英语没有常用的派生形容词或动词。
In modern English, galette is a complete loanword rather than a productive English root-and-affix formation; its plural is galettes. Its French history connects it with galet (pebble) and the -ette form, but this is not an English derivational rule; it has no common derived adjective or verb.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
tart（馅饼）通常指有底、有馅且顶部敞开的点心，常用模具成形；crostata（意式馅饼）与某些 galette 相似，但属于意大利烹饪传统；crêpe（法式薄饼）对应薄饼类，布列塔尼 galette 通常用荞麦制作并配咸馅，而 crêpe 常用小麦粉制作。
Tart usually describes an open pastry with a base and filling, often shaped in a tin; crostata is a similar pastry from the Italian tradition; crêpe overlaps with the pancake sense, but a Breton galette is typically made with buckwheat and served savory, whereas a crêpe commonly uses wheat flour.
