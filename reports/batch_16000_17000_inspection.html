effectual 1 词性 · 双语对齐
GRECEFR-C2
adj. 形容词 //ɪˈfektʃuəl// 有效的；能达到预期目的的 (Successful in producing the intended result.)
COBUILD: If an action or method is effectual, it achieves the result that was intended.
搭配: an effectual remedy; effectual means 有效的补救办法；有效的手段
例句: They could find no effectual means of preventing further damage.
他们找不到能有效防止损害进一步扩大的办法。
🏛️ 来源历史:
经法语 effectuel，源自晚期拉丁语 effectualis，最终与拉丁语 effectus（完成、结果）相连。其核心意义是实际产生所期望的结果。
It came through French effectuel from Late Latin effectualis, ultimately connected with Latin effectus, meaning accomplishment or result. Its central meaning is actually bringing about the desired outcome.
🧩 构词衍生:
现代可分析为 effect（效果）+ -ual（形容词后缀）。主要词族：effectual（有效的）、effectually（有效地）、effectuality（有效性，少用）、ineffectual（无效的）、ineffectually（无效地）、ineffectuality（无效）；相关词有 effect（效果；使发生）、effective（有效的）、effectiveness（有效性）、effectuate（实现）和 effectuation（实现这一行为）。
Modern analysis: effect + the adjective-forming suffix -ual. The main family includes effectual, effectually, effectuality (uncommon), ineffectual, ineffectually, and ineffectuality; related formations include effect, effective, effectiveness, effectuate, and effectuation.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
effective（有效的）最通用；effectual 较正式，突出确实达成目的；efficacious（有功效的）常用于药物、治疗或措施；efficient（高效的）强调节省时间、精力或资源。
Effective is the broad everyday equivalent; effectual is more formal and stresses achieving the intended purpose. Efficacious often describes treatments or remedies, while efficient emphasizes using little time, effort, or resources.
💡 秒记锚点:
effect 是“效果”：effectual 就是能把预期效果真正做出来。
Start with effect: something effectual actually produces the effect you want.

COBUILD: In programming, something that is polymorphic can work with values of different types or provide different behavior through a common interface.
搭配: a polymorphic function; a polymorphic type 多态函数；多态类型
例句: The polymorphic function works with lists containing different types of elements.
这个多态函数可以处理元素类型各不相同的列表。
🏛️ 来源历史:
polymorphic 属于以希腊语 polys（多）和 morphē（形态）为基础形成的科学词族，核心含义是“具有多种形态”。这一概念用于生物学和晶体研究，后来也被计算机科学借用来描述类型或行为的多样性。
Polymorphic belongs to a scientific word family built from Greek polys, 'many,' and morphē, 'form,' expressing the idea of having several forms. The concept was used in biology and crystallography and was later adopted in computing for variation in types or behavior.
🧩 构词衍生:
poly-（多）+ morph（形态）+ -ic（形容词后缀）→ polymorphic。主要同族词包括 polymorph（多形体；多晶型体）、polymorphism（多形性；多晶型性；多态性）、polymorphous（多形的）和 polymorphically（以多形或多态方式）；计算机领域还有 polymorphic type（多态类型）等组合。
Poly- ('many') + morph ('form') + -ic (adjective suffix) → polymorphic. Principal family members include polymorph, polymorphism, polymorphous, and polymorphically; technical combinations include polymorphic type.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
polymorphous（多形的）在一般描述和部分生物学语境中很接近；multiform（多种形式的）较宽泛，通常不表示严格的科学机制；variable（可变的）只强调存在差异或变化，不一定表示多个明确形态。计算机领域的 polymorphic 通常不能随意替换。
Polymorphous is close in general descriptions and some biological contexts; multiform is broader and usually lacks a precise scientific implication; variable indicates differences or change without necessarily implying distinct forms. In computing, polymorphic is a technical term and is generally not interchangeable with these alternatives.
💡 秒记锚点:
poly 是“多”，morph 是“形”：同一类东西，可以有多种表现形式。
Poly means 'many' and morph means 'form': one kind of thing can appear or operate in several forms.
strontium 1 词性 · 双语对齐
n. 名词 //ˈstrɒntiəm// 锶，化学符号为 Sr、原子序数为 38 的金属元素 (The metallic chemical element with symbol Sr and atomic number 38.)
COBUILD: Strontium is a soft, silvery metallic element whose chemical symbol is Sr and whose atomic number is 38.
搭配: strontium salts; strontium isotopes 锶盐；锶同位素
例句: Strontium salts give some fireworks their red colour.
锶盐使某些烟花呈现红色。
🏛️ 来源历史:
strontium 得名于苏格兰村庄 Strontian，当地发现的矿物促成了对这一元素的识别。其名称把地名词干与元素命名后缀 -ium 结合起来。
Strontium is named after Strontian, a Scottish village associated with the mineral that led to the element's identification. Its name combines a stem taken from the place name with the element-naming suffix -ium.
🧩 构词衍生:
构词为 Stront-（取自地名 Strontian）+ -ium（元素名后缀）→ strontium；现代英语通常把它作为完整元素名称使用。主要相关词包括 strontia（氧化锶的传统名称）、strontianite（菱锶矿）及 strontium-bearing（含锶的）；Sr 是化学符号，strontium-90（锶-90）是同位素名称。
The formation is Stront- (from Strontian) + -ium (an element-naming suffix) → strontium; modern English normally treats it as a complete element name. Principal related forms include strontia, a traditional name for strontium oxide; strontianite, the carbonate mineral; and strontium-bearing. Sr is its chemical symbol, and strontium-90 names an isotope.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
strontium 没有常用的真正近义词。Sr 是同一元素的化学符号，strontium metal（金属锶）强调金属形态；calcium（钙）和 barium（钡）虽属于同族元素，却不是锶的同义词。
Strontium has no common true synonym. Sr is the symbol for the same element, while strontium metal emphasizes its metallic form. Calcium and barium are related elements in the same chemical group, not synonyms.
💡 秒记锚点:
地名 Strontian 留下名字，元素后缀 -ium 点明身份：strontium 就是锶。
Strontian supplies the place-name anchor, and -ium signals an element: strontium.
