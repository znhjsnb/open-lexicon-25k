COBUILD: Something that is waterlogged contains so much water that it cannot drain, dry, float, or function properly.
搭配: waterlogged soil; a waterlogged pitch 水分饱和的土壤；积水严重的球场
例句: The match was postponed because the pitch was waterlogged.

🏛️ 来源历史:
waterlogged 由 water（水）与带有“变得像原木一样笨重、难以操控”意味的 logged 结合而成，早期用于描述进水后失去正常浮航能力的船只。后来词义扩展到浸透水的土地、木材及其他物品。
Waterlogged combines water with logged, conveying a log-like, heavy, unmanageable condition, and was first used of vessels disabled by taking in water. Its meaning later extended to saturated ground, timber, and other materials.
🧩 构词衍生:
现代可分析为 water（水）+ log + -ed，整体表示被水浸透的状态；不要把这里的 log 理解为“日志”。常用词族有 waterlogged、waterlog（使浸水；发生水涝）、waterlogs、waterlogging（浸水；水涝）；动词 waterlog 是从较早的 waterlogged 逆构而来的。
The modern form can be analyzed as water + log + -ed, with the whole word describing saturation; log here does not mean a written record. The common family comprises waterlogged, waterlog, waterlogs, and waterlogging; the verb waterlog was back-formed from the earlier adjective waterlogged.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
soaked（湿透的）泛指吸收了很多液体；saturated（饱和的）强调已吸收到容量极限；sodden（湿透而沉重的）常突出湿软、沉重的不适状态。waterlogged 尤其强调水过多，影响排水、使用或浮航。
Soaked broadly means thoroughly wet; saturated emphasizes being filled to capacity; sodden often suggests unpleasant heaviness or softness from moisture. Waterlogged especially highlights excess water that impairs drainage, use, or floating.
💡 秒记锚点:
水多到失去正常功能：土地排不出，船只浮不动。
Too much water stops normal function: soil cannot drain, and a boat cannot float properly.

🧩 构词衍生:
现代英语中 moult 是不可再作常用词缀拆分的词基，可直接用作动词和名词。常用词族为 moult、moults、moulted、moulting；美式拼写对应 molt、molts、molted、molting。moulting 可作分词，也可表示换羽、换毛或蜕皮这一过程。
In modern English, moult is a base without a useful productive prefix-and-suffix breakdown and functions as both verb and noun. Its common family is moult, moults, moulted, and moulting; the American spellings are molt, molts, molted, and molting. Moulting can be a participle or a noun naming the process.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
shed（脱落；脱去）最宽泛，不一定指完整的更新周期；slough（蜕去）常用于蛇皮等外层脱落；ecdysis（蜕皮）是技术性术语，尤其用于节肢动物等的外层脱换。moult 是描述动物周期性换羽、换毛或蜕皮的普通用语。
Shed is broad and need not describe a complete renewal cycle; slough often refers to casting off an outer layer such as snakeskin; ecdysis is a technical term, especially for shedding an external covering in arthropods and other animals. Moult is the ordinary term for an animal's periodic renewal of feathers, hair, or skin.

Support is the broadest term; sawhorse chiefly denotes a frame holding timber for sawing, though it can support a temporary tabletop; framework emphasizes an interconnected structure. Trestle specifically denotes a load-bearing frame and can also name a bridge supported by such frames.

colonnade 借自法语 colonnade，与意大利语 colonnato 及 colonna（柱）有关，最终源于拉丁语 columna（柱）。词义以成排的柱子为核心，并可指这些柱子构成的有顶通道。
Colonnade was borrowed from French colonnade, related to Italian colonnato and colonna, meaning 'column,' ultimately from Latin columna. Its central meaning is a row of columns, and it can also refer to the covered passage they form.
🧩 构词衍生:
现代英语中 colonnade 通常作为借入的整体词基使用；历史上可辨认出表示“柱”的 colonn- 和集合性结尾 -ade，但不宜当作普通英语自由词根的组合。常用词族为 colonnade、colonnades、colonnaded（有柱廊的）；相关同源词包括 column（柱）和 columnar（柱状的）。
In modern English, colonnade is normally treated as a borrowed whole base. Historically, colonn- refers to columns and -ade has a collective function, but this is not an ordinary combination of free English elements. The common family comprises colonnade, colonnades, and colonnaded; related cognates include column and columnar.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
arcade（拱廊）强调连续的拱；portico（门廊）通常是建筑入口处由柱子承托的有顶空间；peristyle（围柱廊）强调环绕庭院或建筑的一圈柱列。colonnade 强调成排的柱子，不要求有拱、位于入口或形成环绕布局。
Arcade emphasizes a series of arches; portico usually denotes a roofed entrance supported by columns; peristyle denotes columns surrounding a courtyard or building. Colonnade emphasizes the row of columns without requiring arches, an entrance position, or a surrounding arrangement.
💡 秒记锚点:

Connect it with column: columns arranged in a row make a colonnade.

A useful modern analysis is de- “remove or reduce” + populate “provide with inhabitants,” although this is not a literal account of the original Latin formation. Related family members include populate, population, populated, repopulate, repopulation, overpopulate, overpopulation, and underpopulated.
⚖️ 近义微雕:

搭配: the penultimate chapter; the penultimate syllable 倒数第二章；倒数第二个音节

The word comes from Latin paenultimus, combining paene “almost” and ultimus “last,” literally “almost last.” It retains this positional meaning: immediately before the final item.
🧩 构词衍生:
现代主要作为完整形容词使用；可借历史结构 pen-（几乎）+ ultimate（最后的）理解，但 pen- 不是现代英语中能自由造词的常用前缀。派生及相关词：penultimately（在倒数第二位地，较少用）、penult／penultima（倒数第二个音节）；antepenultimate（倒数第三的）属于相关构词。ultimate（最后的、最终的）和 ultimately（最终）属于同源词族。
In modern English, this is mainly used as a whole adjective. Its historical structure can be understood as pen- “almost” + ultimate “last,” but pen- is not a freely productive modern prefix. Derivatives and related forms include penultimately, an uncommon adverb; penult and penultima, meaning the second-to-last syllable; and antepenultimate, meaning third-to-last. Ultimate and ultimately belong to the same historical family.
⚖️ 近义微雕:

Second-to-last, next-to-last, and last but one all express the same position. Second-to-last and next-to-last are more conversational, last but one is especially associated with British English, and penultimate is more compact and formal.

由 autonomy 的词干加 -ic 构成；autonomy 最终源于希腊语 autonomia，含有 autos（自己）和 nomos（法则、规则），原意为“自我治理”。该词后来用于身体的自主调节，计算机领域又借用这一概念表示系统自主管理。
Autonomic was formed from the stem of autonomy with -ic; autonomy ultimately comes from Greek autonomia, combining autos “self” and nomos “law or rule.” The idea of self-government was applied to bodily regulation and later borrowed in computing for systems that manage themselves.
🧩 构词衍生:
autonomy 去掉词尾 -y，再加形容词后缀 -ic → autonomic。直接派生词 autonomically（通过自主神经调节地；以自主管理方式）；相关词族包括 autonomy（自主权、自主性）、autonomous（自主的、自治的）、autonomously（自主地）、autonomist（自治主义者）和 autonomism（自治主义）。autonomous 与 autonomic 意义相关，但在神经科学中不能任意互换。
Autonomy loses final -y and takes the adjective suffix -ic to form autonomic. Its direct adverbial derivative is autonomically. Related family members include autonomy, autonomous, autonomously, autonomist, and autonomism. Autonomous and autonomic share the idea of self-direction, but they are not interchangeable in neuroscience.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
involuntary（不随意的）强调不受意识控制，范围不限于自主神经活动；automatic（自动的）强调无需主动操作或思考；autonomous（自主的）强调独立行动或管理的能力。autonomic 在生理学中专指自主神经调节，在计算机领域强调系统自主管理。
Involuntary emphasizes absence of conscious control and extends beyond autonomic activity; automatic emphasizes operation without deliberate action or thought; autonomous emphasizes independent action or management. Autonomic specifically concerns autonomic nervous regulation in physiology and self-management in computing.
💡 秒记锚点:
“自己定规则、自己运行”：身体自动调节，系统自主管理。
“Self-rule in action”: the body regulates itself, and a system manages itself.
diode 1 词性 · 双语对齐
TOEFLGRECEFR-C2
n. 名词 //ˈdaɪəʊd// 二极管；通常主要允许电流沿一个方向通过的双端电子元件 (A two-terminal electronic component that typically conducts current mainly in one direction.)
COBUILD: A diode is an electronic component with two terminals that usually allows current to flow much more easily in one direction than in the other.
搭配: a semiconductor diode; a light-emitting diode 半导体二极管；发光二极管
例句: The diode allows current to flow through the circuit in only one direction under normal operating conditions.
在正常工作条件下，该二极管只允许电流沿一个方向流过电路。
🏛️ 来源历史:
这是现代电子学术语，由希腊语来源的 di-（二）与从 electrode 等电学术语中抽取的 -ode 组合而成。名称最初突出双电极结构，后来也用于半导体二极管。
Diode is a modern electronics term combining Greek-derived di- “two” with -ode, taken from electrical terms such as electrode. The name originally highlighted a two-electrode structure and was later applied to semiconductor devices.
🧩 构词衍生:
di-（二）+ -ode（见于电极相关术语的构词成分）→ diode；复数 diodes。常见复合词族包括 photodiode（光电二极管）、light-emitting diode／LED（发光二极管）、laser diode（激光二极管）、Zener diode（齐纳二极管、稳压二极管）和 rectifier diode（整流二极管）。这些主要是复合术语，而非后缀派生词。
Di- “two” + -ode, a combining element found in electrode-related terminology, forms diode; the plural is diodes. Common compound family members include photodiode, light-emitting diode or LED, laser diode, Zener diode, and rectifier diode. These are mainly compound technical terms rather than suffixal derivatives.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
没有覆盖 diode 全部含义的常用精确同义词。rectifier（整流器）强调把交流转换为直流的功能，可能由多个二极管组成；semiconductor diode（半导体二极管）限定器件材料类型；two-terminal device（双端器件）范围更广，也包括电阻等其他元件。
There is no common exact synonym covering every kind of diode. Rectifier emphasizes converting alternating current to direct current and may contain several diodes; semiconductor diode specifies a material-based subtype; two-terminal device is broader and also includes components such as resistors.
💡 秒记锚点:
di- 记“两端”，功能联想“电流单向阀”。
Di- gives you two terminals; picture a one-way valve for current.
