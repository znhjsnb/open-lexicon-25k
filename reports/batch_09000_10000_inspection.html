COBUILD: If the authorities cordon off an area, they surround it with officers or barriers to stop people from entering.

🏛️ 来源历史:

Cordon was borrowed from French, where it meant a small cord or ribbon and was a diminutive of corde, meaning rope. In English, its meaning extended to a surrounding line of people or barriers, and it subsequently became a verb.

In modern English, cordon is a base without a productive internal affix division; the noun becomes a verb through conversion. Its family and related forms include cordons, cordoned, and cordoning; cordon off is a phrasal verb, with off functioning as a particle rather than a suffix.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
barrier（屏障）泛指阻碍通行的事物；perimeter（周界）强调区域的外缘，本身不一定有警戒人员；seal off（封锁）强调阻止出入的结果。cordon／cordon off 更突出以人员或障碍物围出警戒范围。
Barrier is a broad term for something that blocks passage; perimeter names an area's outer boundary without necessarily implying guards; seal off emphasizes preventing access. Cordon and cordon off specifically suggest a surrounding security line of people or barriers.
💡 秒记锚点:

A useful root-based analysis is ir-, the assimilated form of in- before r, plus radi-, ray, plus the verbal ending -ate. 搭配: a sociable evening; sociable working hours 一个愉快相聚的夜晚；便于社交的工作时间
例句: Her new job offers more sociable working hours.
她的新工作时间更便于她与亲友相聚。
🏛️ 来源历史:
经法语 sociable 或直接由拉丁语 sociabilis 进入英语，最终与 socius（伙伴、同伴）有关。词义从“适合结伴、交往”发展为“乐于与人相处”，也用于描述便于交往的活动或安排。
Sociable entered English through French sociable or directly from Latin sociabilis, ultimately related to socius, meaning companion. Its meanings developed from being suited to companionship or association to enjoying company, and also to describing activities or arrangements that encourage it.
🧩 构词衍生:
可按词族理解为 soci-（伙伴、交往）+ -able（具有……性质的），但不是把独立英语单词直接拼接而成。常用词族：sociability（好交际；合群）、sociably（合群地）、unsociable（不合群的；不便社交的）、unsociably（不合群地）和 unsociability（不合群）；相关同根词有 social（社会的；社交的）和 society（社会）。
Within its word family, sociable can be analyzed as soci-, relating to companionship or association, plus adjectival -able, though it is not formed by joining two independent English words. Common family members are sociability, sociably, unsociable, unsociably, and unsociability; related words sharing the historical root include social and society.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
friendly（友好的）侧重待人友善，不一定喜欢频繁交往；outgoing（外向的）侧重主动、自信地与人接触；gregarious（爱群居、爱热闹的）更强调喜欢人多的陪伴。sociable 的核心是乐于与人交往。
Friendly emphasizes kindness and warmth without necessarily implying frequent socializing; outgoing emphasizes confidence and initiative in meeting people; gregarious more strongly suggests enjoying groups and plentiful company. Sociable centers on enjoying interaction with others.
💡 秒记锚点:
soci- 联想到“伙伴”：喜欢有伙伴相伴，就是 sociable。
Connect soci- with companion: someone who enjoys having companions is sociable.
tectonic 2 词性 · 双语对齐
TEM8IELTSTOEFLGRECEFR-C2
adj. 形容词 //tekˈtɒnɪk// 地壳构造的；与地球岩石圈结构及运动有关的 (relating to the structure and movement of Earth's crust and lithosphere)
COBUILD: Tectonic processes and movements affect the structure of the Earth's outer layers, including the movement of its plates.
搭配: tectonic plates; tectonic activity 构造板块；构造活动
例句: The movement of tectonic plates can cause earthquakes.
构造板块的运动可能引发地震。
adj. 形容词 //tekˈtɒnɪk// 引起根本性变化的；影响深远的 (involving a profound change in an underlying system or structure)
COBUILD: A tectonic change is a very large change that transforms the basic structure of a situation or system.
搭配: a tectonic shift; tectonic changes 一次根本性转变；深刻的结构性变化
例句: The internet brought a tectonic shift in how people access information.
互联网使人们获取信息的方式发生了根本性转变。
🏛️ 来源历史:
源自希腊语 tektonikos（建造的），与 tektōn（建造者、木匠）有关，经拉丁语形式传入后来的欧洲语言。英语中原与建造或结构有关，后来用于地质构造，并由地壳运动引申出“造成深刻结构性变化的”这一比喻义。
Tectonic derives from Greek tektonikos, meaning relating to building, from tektōn, a builder or carpenter, transmitted through a Latin form. In English, its connection with construction and structure developed into a geological meaning, which later inspired the figurative sense of profound structural change.
🧩 构词衍生:
tecton-（建造、结构）+ -ic（形容词后缀）→ tectonic。主要词族有 tectonics（构造地质学；构造作用）、tectonically（在构造上；从构造地质学角度）、tectonism（构造作用）；专业扩展有 neotectonic（新构造的）和 neotectonics（新构造学）。plate tectonics 是“板块构造学”。
Tecton-, meaning building or structure, plus adjectival -ic forms tectonic. Principal family members include tectonics, the study or processes of geological structure; tectonically, an adverb; and tectonism, tectonic deformation or activity. Technical extensions include neotectonic and neotectonics. Plate tectonics names the theory of moving lithospheric plates.
⚖️ 近义微雕:
crustal（地壳的）侧重所属部位；structural（结构的）范围更广，不一定涉及地质运动；seismic（地震的；震撼性的）侧重地震或其比喻性冲击。tectonic 强调深层结构及其运动或改变。
Crustal specifies a relationship to Earth's crust; structural is broader and need not involve geological movement; seismic emphasizes earthquakes or, figuratively, a dramatic impact. Tectonic emphasizes underlying structures and their movement or transformation.
💡 秒记锚点:
从“建造者”到“地球结构”：板块一动，结构改变；比喻义也抓住“根本改变”。
Move from builder to Earth's structure: moving plates reshape the structure, and figurative tectonic shifts likewise transform the foundations.
